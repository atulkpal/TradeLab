# Lecture 5.7.3: Combating FOMO and Greed

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.7: FOMO & Greed Biases | **Lecture:** 5.7.3

**Concept:** FOMO and greed lead to irrational decisions.

---

The best defense is a well-defined trading plan. By having a plan, you know exactly when to enter and exit. You also know how much you are willing to risk. Sticking to your plan removes the influence of these emotions. Maintaining a trading journal also helps you identify patterns of FOMO and greed in your trading. A disciplined approach is your best armor against these powerful biases. In India, successful traders like Radhakishan Damani are known for their disciplined, emotionless approach.
