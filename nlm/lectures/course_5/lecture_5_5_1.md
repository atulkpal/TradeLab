# Lecture 5.5.1: What is a Drawdown?

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.5: Drawdowns & Recovery Math | **Lecture:** 5.5.1

**Concept:** Drawdowns are inevitable; recovery requires discipline.

---

A drawdown is the decline in your trading account balance from its peak to its trough. It is a measure of risk. A drawdown of 20% means your account has fallen 20% from its highest point. Drawdowns are a normal and inevitable part of trading. The key is not whether you will have drawdowns, but how severe they will be and how you respond to them. A well-managed account experiences smaller and less frequent drawdowns. In India, even the best traders face drawdowns during market corrections.
