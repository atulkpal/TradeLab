# Lecture 5.9.1: The Concept of a Discipline Score

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.9: Discipline Score & Habit Loops | **Lecture:** 5.9.1

**Concept:** Track your discipline to build positive habit loops.

---

A Discipline Score is a self-assessment tool that tracks how well you adhered to your trading plan. You give yourself a score from 1 to 10 after each trading day or week. You deduct points for violations like not using a stop-loss, deviating from your plan, or revenge trading. This system makes your discipline measurable and holds you accountable. A high Discipline Score often correlates with better long-term trading results. Many Indian traders use this technique to build consistent habits.
