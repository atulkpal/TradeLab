# Lecture 5.7.1: Understanding FOMO

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.7: FOMO & Greed Biases | **Lecture:** 5.7.1

**Concept:** FOMO and greed lead to irrational decisions.

---

FOMO, or Fear Of Missing Out, is the feeling of anxiety that you are missing a profitable opportunity. It often happens when you see a stock making a sharp move without you. FOMO causes you to abandon your trading plan and chase trades at bad prices. It leads to entering a trade near the top of a rally, increasing the risk of a sudden reversal. In the Indian market, FOMO is common during strong sector rallies like the IT boom in 2020 or the recent defense stock rallies.
