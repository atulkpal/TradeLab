# Lecture 5.5.3: Psychological Impact of Drawdowns

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.5: Drawdowns & Recovery Math | **Lecture:** 5.5.3

**Concept:** Drawdowns are inevitable; recovery requires discipline.

---

Drawdowns take a heavy psychological toll. They can lead to doubt, fear, and a loss of confidence. This often leads to 'drawdown paralysis,' where a trader stops trading or makes reckless attempts to 'get back' the lost money, leading to more losses. The best way to handle drawdowns is to reduce your position size, review your trades, and stick to your plan. It is a test of your emotional discipline. Many Indian traders give up after their first major drawdown, but professionals use it as a learning opportunity.
