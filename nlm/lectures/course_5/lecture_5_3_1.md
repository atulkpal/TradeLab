# Lecture 5.3.1: The Purpose of a Stop-Loss

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.3: Stop-Loss & Trailing Stops | **Lecture:** 5.3.1

**Concept:** Stop-losses limit losses; trailing stops protect profits.

---

A stop-loss is an order placed with your broker to buy or sell a stock once the price reaches a specific level. Its purpose is to limit your potential loss on a trade. It is a mandatory tool for risk management. Without a stop-loss, a single adverse move can cause devastating losses. Deciding where to place your stop-loss is an art that involves understanding market structure, support/resistance, and your risk tolerance. It is your exit plan. In India, all professional traders use stop-losses on every trade.
