# Lecture 5.2.3: The Kelly Criterion

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.2: Position Sizing Models | **Lecture:** 5.2.3

**Concept:** Models like the 1% rule help you determine how much to risk.

---

The Kelly Criterion is a mathematical formula used to determine the optimal size of a series of bets. It is defined as: f* = (bp - q) / b, where 'p' is the probability of winning, 'q' is the probability of losing, and 'b' is the odds received. In trading, it can be used to maximize the growth of capital. However, it is very aggressive and can lead to large drawdowns. It is often recommended to use a 'Half-Kelly' to be more conservative. In India, very few retail traders use this due to its complexity.
