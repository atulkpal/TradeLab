# Lecture 5.1.3: Changing Your Mindset

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.1: Why Risk Comes First | **Lecture:** 5.1.3

**Concept:** Protecting your capital is more important than maximizing returns.

---

Most retail traders focus on 'how much can I make?' A risk-first mindset asks: 'How much can I lose?' This shift in perspective changes everything. It makes you plan your trade and trade your plan. It forces you to ask: 'What if I'm wrong?' and 'Where is my exit?' This discipline separates the amateurs from the professionals. The market doesn't care about your opinion; it cares about your risk management. In India, successful traders like Rakesh Jhunjhunwala emphasized capital protection above all else.
