# Lecture 5.2.2: The Fixed Ratio Method

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.2: Position Sizing Models | **Lecture:** 5.2.2

**Concept:** Models like the 1% rule help you determine how much to risk.

---

The Fixed Ratio method increases your position size after a certain profit level is reached, rather than just as a percentage of capital. It is a more aggressive approach that attempts to grow the position during winning streaks. It is more complex and requires you to define a 'delta' (the amount of profit needed to increase the position size). This is a more advanced model for traders with a proven track record. It's not recommended for beginners in the Indian market.
