# Lecture 5.4.2: RRR and Win Rate

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.4: Risk-to-Reward Ratio | **Lecture:** 5.4.2

**Concept:** RRR compares the potential profit to the potential loss.

---

There is a direct relationship between RRR and the win rate required to be profitable. If you have a 1:2 RRR (risk 1, gain 2), you only need a win rate of 34% to break even. If you have a 1:1 RRR, you need a 50% win rate. A favorable RRR allows you to be less accurate in your predictions but still be profitable. This is why many professional traders focus on RRR over win rate. In India, traders often use this to justify taking trades with lower probability.
