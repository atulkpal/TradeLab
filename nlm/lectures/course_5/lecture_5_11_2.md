# Lecture 5.11.2: The Benefits of Taking a Break

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.11: Knowing When to Step Away | **Lecture:** 5.11.2

**Concept:** Stepping away preserves capital and mental health.

---

Stepping away from the market allows you to gain a fresh perspective. It helps you avoid emotional decisions and reduces the risk of making mistakes. It also gives you time to review your trading plan and journal. A break of a few days can often lead to clearer thinking and better decision-making when you return. It is a crucial part of maintaining a healthy trading mindset. Many successful Indian traders take regular breaks from the markets.
