# Lecture 5.8.3: How to Stop the Cycle

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.8: Revenge Trading & Overtrading | **Lecture:** 5.8.3

**Concept:** Revenge trading is emotional; overtrading is volume-based.

---

The first step is to recognize the symptoms. If you feel a strong urge to immediately jump into a trade after a loss, stop. Walk away from your screen. Accept the loss as a cost of doing business. Stick to your daily loss limit. For overtrading, set a maximum number of trades per day. Focus on the quality of the setup, not the quantity of trades. In India, many traders use a 'loss limit' to prevent revenge trading and overtrading.
