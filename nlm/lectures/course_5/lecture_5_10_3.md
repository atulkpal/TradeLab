# Lecture 5.10.3: The Weekly Review

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.10: Journaling & Trade Review | **Lecture:** 5.10.3

**Concept:** Journaling helps you learn from your past trades.

---

Set aside time weekly to review your journal. Look for patterns in your winning and losing trades. Are your wins coming from a specific setup? Are your losses coming from a specific emotional state? This review helps you refine your strategy and eliminate weaknesses. It is the process of continuous improvement that separates successful traders from the rest. In India, traders who regularly review their journals tend to improve their profitability over time.
