# Lecture 5.6.2: Why 1%?

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.6: The 1% Rule | **Lecture:** 5.6.2

**Concept:** Risking only 1% of capital per trade ensures long-term survival.

---

Risking 1% per trade makes the math work in your favor. If you have a losing streak of 10 trades, your maximum drawdown is only 10%. This is easily recoverable. It also reduces emotional stress because the loss on any single trade is small enough not to cause panic. This allows you to stick to your trading plan with confidence. Many professional traders use even less, such as 0.5%. In India, traders with smaller capital often struggle with this rule but it's essential for longevity.
