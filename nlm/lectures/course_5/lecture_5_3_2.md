# Lecture 5.3.2: Types of Stop-Losses

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.3: Stop-Loss & Trailing Stops | **Lecture:** 5.3.2

**Concept:** Stop-losses limit losses; trailing stops protect profits.

---

There are different types. A 'fixed stop-loss' is set at a specific price level. A 'trailing stop-loss' moves in your favor as the price moves in your direction. For example, a 5% trailing stop on a stock that moves up 10% will lock in a 5% profit. Trailing stops are excellent for letting winners run while protecting against a sudden reversal. In India, you can also use Good Till Triggered (GTT) orders to create long-term stop-losses. Each type has its use case depending on your strategy.
