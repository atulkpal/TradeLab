# Lecture 5.8.2: What is Overtrading?

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.8: Revenge Trading & Overtrading | **Lecture:** 5.8.2

**Concept:** Revenge trading is emotional; overtrading is volume-based.

---

Overtrading is trading too frequently, or trading with too large a position size relative to your capital. It can be driven by excitement, boredom, or a desire to make quick money. Overtrading increases your transaction costs and exposes you to unnecessary risk. It dilutes the quality of your trades. Often, a trader makes 10 trades when they should have only made 2. In India, overtrading is common among intraday traders who feel the need to be 'active' in the market.
