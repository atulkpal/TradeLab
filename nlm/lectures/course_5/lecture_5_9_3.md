# Lecture 5.9.3: Building Good Trading Habits

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.9: Discipline Score & Habit Loops | **Lecture:** 5.9.3

**Concept:** Track your discipline to build positive habit loops.

---

Building good trading habits takes time and repetition. Start by defining the habits you want to build (e.g., always check your RRR, always place a stop-loss). Then, consciously practice them until they become automatic. Use a checklist before every trade. The goal is to make good trading a habit, not just a conscious effort. This is the hallmark of a professional trader. In India, traders who maintain a trade checklist tend to perform better over time.
