# Lecture 5.1.2: The Math of Recovery

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.1: Why Risk Comes First | **Lecture:** 5.1.2

**Concept:** Protecting your capital is more important than maximizing returns.

---

Losses are mathematically harder to recover from. If you lose 50% of your capital, you need a 100% return just to get back to break-even. This is a powerful reason to avoid large drawdowns. A disciplined trader focuses on making consistent small gains while avoiding large losses. This 'compound returns' approach is what builds sustainable wealth over time.
