# Lecture 5.9.2: Understanding Habit Loops

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.9: Discipline Score & Habit Loops | **Lecture:** 5.9.2

**Concept:** Track your discipline to build positive habit loops.

---

Habit loops consist of a cue, a routine, and a reward. In trading, a 'cue' could be a price movement, the 'routine' is your response to it, and the 'reward' is the dopamine hit you get from being in a trade. To build good habits, you need to make the routine a positive, disciplined action. For example, if you see a potential trade (cue), your routine should be to review your plan (routine), not to immediately enter the trade. This is key to long-term trading success.
