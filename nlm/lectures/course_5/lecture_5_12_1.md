# Lecture 5.12.1: The Components of a Trading Plan

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.12: Building a Personal Trading Plan | **Lecture:** 5.12.1

**Concept:** A trading plan is your rules-based guide for all trading decisions.

---

A trading plan is a comprehensive document that outlines all the rules and parameters of your trading. It includes your financial goals, risk tolerance, the markets you trade, your trading strategy (entry and exit rules), position sizing rules, and daily loss limits. It is your guidebook. It forces you to think through all aspects of your trading before you start. It is the single most important document for any trader. In India, every serious trader should have a written trading plan.
