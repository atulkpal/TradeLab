# Lecture 5.11.3: Practical Steps

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.11: Knowing When to Step Away | **Lecture:** 5.11.3

**Concept:** Stepping away preserves capital and mental health.

---

Set rules for yourself. For example, if you have two consecutive losing trades, take a break for an hour. If you hit your daily loss limit, step away for the day. If you feel emotionally compromised, close all positions and take a few days off. These are not signs of failure; they are signs of a disciplined trader who respects the market and their own mental well-being. In India, traders often use a 'daily loss limit' as a key risk management tool.
