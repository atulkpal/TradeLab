# Lecture 5.8.1: What is Revenge Trading?

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.8: Revenge Trading & Overtrading | **Lecture:** 5.8.1

**Concept:** Revenge trading is emotional; overtrading is volume-based.

---

Revenge trading is the act of trying to immediately recover a loss by making impulsive, often larger, trades. It is driven by a desire to 'get back' at the market. This is a destructive cycle: you lose, you trade aggressively to recover the loss, you lose more, and the cycle continues. It is a sign that emotion has taken over your decision-making. Revenge trading often leads to much larger losses than the initial one. In India, this is one of the leading causes of retail trader failure.
