# Lecture 5.4.3: Using RRR in Planning

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.4: Risk-to-Reward Ratio | **Lecture:** 5.4.3

**Concept:** RRR compares the potential profit to the potential loss.

---

Before entering any trade, you should know your entry price, your stop-loss level, and your target price. If the RRR is not favorable (less than 1:2), it is often better to skip the trade. This discipline prevents you from taking low-quality setups. In the Indian market, with high transaction costs (STT, brokerage, GST), aiming for a higher RRR (like 1:3) is often advisable to cover these costs. Always calculate your RRR before placing any trade.
