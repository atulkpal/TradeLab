# Lecture 5.12.3: Reviewing and Updating Your Plan

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.12: Building a Personal Trading Plan | **Lecture:** 5.12.3

**Concept:** A trading plan is your rules-based guide for all trading decisions.

---

Your trading plan is not static. As you gain experience and your capital grows, your plan should evolve. You should review it regularly (e.g., quarterly) to see what's working and what isn't. Did you achieve your goals? Are there areas to improve? Updating your plan based on data from your trading journal is a sign of a maturing trader. It is a living document that reflects your growth. In India, traders who adapt their plans to changing market conditions tend to survive longer.
