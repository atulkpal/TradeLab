# Lecture 5.5.2: The Math of Recovery

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.5: Drawdowns & Recovery Math | **Lecture:** 5.5.2

**Concept:** Drawdowns are inevitable; recovery requires discipline.

---

As discussed earlier, the math of recovery is challenging. A 20% drawdown requires a 25% gain to recover to the peak. A 50% drawdown requires a 100% gain. This is why protecting against large drawdowns is so critical. The deeper the drawdown, the harder and longer it takes to recover. A trader who suffers a 50% drawdown needs to double their money, which is much harder than making a 20% gain. This is why risk management is non-negotiable.
