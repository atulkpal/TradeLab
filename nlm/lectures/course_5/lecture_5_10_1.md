# Lecture 5.10.1: The Purpose of a Trading Journal

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.10: Journaling & Trade Review | **Lecture:** 5.10.1

**Concept:** Journaling helps you learn from your past trades.

---

A trading journal is the most powerful tool for a trader's development. It is a record of all your trades, including entry and exit prices, position size, and the reasons for taking the trade. It also includes your emotional state at the time of the trade. The purpose is to provide data to analyze your performance objectively. It turns your trading history into a learning tool. Without a journal, you are likely to repeat the same mistakes. In India, many successful traders credit their journals for their success.
