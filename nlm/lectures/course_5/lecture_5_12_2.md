# Lecture 5.12.2: Why You Need a Plan

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.12: Building a Personal Trading Plan | **Lecture:** 5.12.2

**Concept:** A trading plan is your rules-based guide for all trading decisions.

---

Without a plan, you are trading on emotion and impulse. A plan provides structure and discipline. It removes ambiguity and ensures you are consistent in your approach. It also allows you to evaluate your performance objectively. A plan is your defense against the psychological biases we've discussed. Every professional trader has a plan. In India, traders who follow a plan consistently outperform those who don't, regardless of their strategy.
