# Lecture 5.4.1: Calculating the RRR

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.4: Risk-to-Reward Ratio | **Lecture:** 5.4.1

**Concept:** RRR compares the potential profit to the potential loss.

---

The Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR) is the ratio of potential loss to potential gain on a trade. It is calculated as (Entry Price - Stop-Loss) / (Target - Entry Price). RRR is a key component of any trading plan. In India, traders often aim for 1:3 or higher to cover transaction costs.
