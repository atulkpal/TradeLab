# Lecture 5.10.2: What to Record

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.10: Journaling & Trade Review | **Lecture:** 5.10.2

**Concept:** Journaling helps you learn from your past trades.

---

A good journal should include: the date and time, the stock or index, your entry and exit price, the stop-loss level, the position size, the RRR, and the outcome (profit/loss). Critically, include the 'why'-why you took the trade (setup, rationale) and how you felt (confident, anxious). This qualitative data is incredibly valuable for identifying psychological patterns. For Indian traders, recording the time of day and market conditions can also provide useful insights.
