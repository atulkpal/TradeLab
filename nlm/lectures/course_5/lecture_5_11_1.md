# Lecture 5.11.1: Recognizing the Signs

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.11: Knowing When to Step Away | **Lecture:** 5.11.1

**Concept:** Stepping away preserves capital and mental health.

---

Knowing when to step away from the market is a key skill. Signs include: feeling emotional about your trades, feeling compelled to chase a loss, being unable to focus, or feeling tired. Trading requires high cognitive function and emotional control. When you are tired, stressed, or distracted, you are more likely to make mistakes. Recognizing these signs and acting on them is a sign of strength, not weakness. In India, many traders ignore these signs and suffer the consequences.
