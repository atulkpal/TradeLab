# Lecture 5.7.2: Greed and Overconfidence

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.7: FOMO & Greed Biases | **Lecture:** 5.7.2

**Concept:** FOMO and greed lead to irrational decisions.

---

Greed is the desire for excessive gains. It often manifests after a series of wins, leading to overconfidence and taking on too much risk. Greed makes you hold a winning trade too long, watching your profits evaporate. It also makes you increase your position size beyond your risk limits, exposing you to more risk. Greed is a powerful emotion that must be recognized and managed. In India, many traders who made quick profits during COVID-19 markets later lost everything due to greed.
