# Lecture 5.1.1: The First Rule of Trading

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.1: Why Risk Comes First | **Lecture:** 5.1.1

**Concept:** Protecting your capital is more important than maximizing returns.

---

The first rule of trading is: don't lose money. The second rule is: don't forget the first rule. This means that capital preservation is always the top priority. Your primary job as a trader is not to make profits; it is to survive. Profit will follow if you manage risk. A single large loss can wipe out weeks or months of gains. The psychology of protecting capital is the foundation of a successful and long-lasting trading career. In India, many retail traders fail because they ignore this fundamental rule.
