# Lecture 5.3.3: Psychological Impact of Stop-Losses

**Course:** 5 | **Chapter:** Chapter 5.3: Stop-Loss & Trailing Stops | **Lecture:** 5.3.3

**Concept:** Stop-losses limit losses; trailing stops protect profits.

---

Emotionally, placing a stop-loss is difficult. It forces you to admit you could be wrong. Many novice traders avoid stop-losses, hoping a losing trade will turn around, often leading to much larger losses. Using a stop-loss removes emotion from the exit decision. It is a mechanical process that ensures you manage your risk objectively. A stop-loss is not a loss; it is the cost of doing business. In India, traders often place stop-losses just below support levels for optimal risk management.
