# Lecture 6.10.1: Choosing the Wrong ITR Form

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.10: Common Filing Mistakes | **Lecture:** 6.10.1

**Concept:** Avoid common errors to prevent tax notices.

---

One of the most common mistakes is filing the wrong ITR form. For example, a trader with business income might file ITR-2 (meant for capital gains) instead of ITR-3. This mismatch results in a defective return. The Income Tax Department will issue a notice, and you will have to file a revised return. Always consult a CA or use a reliable tax software to ensure you are using the correct form. This is tax information, not professional advice.
