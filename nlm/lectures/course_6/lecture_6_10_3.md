# Lecture 6.10.3: Missing the Deadline for Loss Carry Forward

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.10: Common Filing Mistakes | **Lecture:** 6.10.3

**Concept:** Avoid common errors to prevent tax notices.

---

As mentioned, to carry forward a loss, you must file your return by the due date. Many traders miss this and lose the benefit of carrying forward their losses. This is a costly mistake. The due date for filing ITR is July 31st (or October 31st if you need a tax audit). Mark your calendar and file on time to protect your tax-saving options. This is tax information, not professional advice.
