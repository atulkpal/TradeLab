# Lecture 6.8.1: Set-off of Losses

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.8: Set-off & Carry Forward of Losses | **Lecture:** 6.8.1

**Concept:** Losses can be set off against other gains or carried forward.

---

Set-off means adjusting a loss from one source against income from another source in the same financial year. This reduces your total taxable income. Capital losses are set off against capital gains. A short-term capital loss can be set off against both short-term and long-term capital gains. A long-term capital loss can only be set off against long-term capital gains. Business losses (non-speculative) can be set off against any other business income, salary, or capital gains. This is tax information, not professional advice.
