# Lecture 6.7.2: Key Schedules for Traders and Investors

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.7: Filing Basics - ITR Forms & Schedules | **Lecture:** 6.7.2

**Concept:** Different ITR forms are for different income types.

---

For traders, Schedule BP is where you report your business income and deductions. Schedule CFL is for reporting your capital gains. For investors, Schedule CG is the critical schedule. It details your short-term and long-term capital gains. Additionally, you must report all your assets and liabilities in Schedule AL. It is a good practice to have all your trade data ready before you start filling out these forms. This is tax information, not professional advice.
