# Lecture 6.9.1: The Presumptive Taxation Scheme

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.9: Options & Futures Tax Treatment | **Lecture:** 6.9.1

**Concept:** F&O trading is generally treated as business income.

---

Many F&O traders in India opt for the presumptive taxation scheme under Section 44AD. Under this scheme, you can declare a profit of 6% of your turnover (or 8% if it's not on a digital platform) and pay tax on that instead of maintaining detailed books.
