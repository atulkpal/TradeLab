# Lecture 6.6.1: What is Turnover?

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.6: Turnover & Bookkeeping for Traders | **Lecture:** 6.6.1

**Concept:** Turnover is not profit; proper bookkeeping is mandatory.

---

Turnover is the total value of all your transactions, not your profit. For a trader, calculating turnover is essential for tax audit purposes and for paying GST. For equity delivery, turnover is the total of all sales (or purchases, whichever is higher). For intraday trades, turnover is the absolute profit (or loss) of all trades. For F&O, turnover is the total of all positive and negative differences. Understanding this is crucial for compliance. This is tax information, not professional advice.
