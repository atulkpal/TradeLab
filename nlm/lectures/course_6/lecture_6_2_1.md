# Lecture 6.2.1: Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.2: STT, Brokerage & Transaction Charges | **Lecture:** 6.2.1

**Concept:** STT is a tax on sale; brokerage is the fee to the broker.

---

STT is a tax levied by the central government on the sale of securities traded on the stock exchanges. It is a direct cost to the trader and is automatically deducted at the time of the transaction. The rate varies depending on the type of transaction. For example, STT on a delivery sale is 0.1% of the total turnover. For F&O, STT is very low (0.05% on option premiums and 0.01% on futures sales). STT is a major cost, especially for high-volume traders in India.
