# Lecture 6.7.1: Which ITR Form to Use

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.7: Filing Basics - ITR Forms & Schedules | **Lecture:** 6.7.1

**Concept:** Different ITR forms are for different income types.

---

The Income Tax Return (ITR) form you use depends on your income source. For a trader with business income, you typically use ITR-3. This form covers business and profession income. For an investor with only capital gains, you would use ITR-2. If you only have salary and interest income, you use ITR-1. It is crucial to choose the correct form. Filing the wrong form can lead to processing delays and notices from the Income Tax Department. This is tax information, not professional advice.
