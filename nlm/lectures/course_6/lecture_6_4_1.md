# Lecture 6.4.1: The Holding Period

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.4: Short-Term vs Long-Term Capital Gains | **Lecture:** 6.4.1

**Concept:** Holding period determines STCG vs LTCG and tax rates.

---

The classification of your gains as short-term or long-term depends on the holding period of the asset. For listed equity shares and equity-oriented mutual funds, the holding period is 12 months. If you hold the asset for 12 months or less, the gain is Short-Term Capital Gain (STCG). If you hold it for more than 12 months, it is Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG). This distinction is crucial because of the significant difference in tax rates. This is tax information, not professional advice.
