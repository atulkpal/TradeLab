# Lecture 6.8.2: Carry Forward of Losses

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.8: Set-off & Carry Forward of Losses | **Lecture:** 6.8.2

**Concept:** Losses can be set off against other gains or carried forward.

---

If you cannot fully set off a loss in the current year, you can 'carry forward' the balance to the next financial year. Speculative losses can be carried forward for 4 years. Capital losses (STCG and LTCG) can be carried forward for 8 years. Non-speculative business losses can be carried forward for 8 years. However, to carry forward a loss, you must file your income tax return on time (before the due date). This is a critical rule. This is tax information, not professional advice.
