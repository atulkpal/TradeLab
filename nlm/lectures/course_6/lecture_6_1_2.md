# Lecture 6.1.2: Business Income vs. Capital Gains

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.1: Trading vs Investing - Different Tax Treatments | **Lecture:** 6.1.2

**Concept:** Trading profits are business income; investing profits are capital gains.

---

Business income from trading is taxed as 'speculative business income' (for intraday F&O) or 'non-speculative business income' (for delivery trading). You can deduct all business expenses related to your trading. Capital gains are classified into Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) and Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG).
