# Lecture 6.4.3: Tax-Loss Harvesting

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.4: Short-Term vs Long-Term Capital Gains | **Lecture:** 6.4.3

**Concept:** Holding period determines STCG vs LTCG and tax rates.

---

Tax-loss harvesting is a strategy to reduce your tax liability. It involves selling securities at a loss to offset capital gains. You can set off short-term capital losses against both short-term and long-term capital gains. Long-term capital losses can only be set off against long-term capital gains. This is a legal and effective way to minimize your tax outgo and is commonly used by smart investors. This is tax information, not professional advice.
