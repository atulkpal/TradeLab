# Lecture 6.3.2: Stamp Duty on Transfer

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.3: GST & Stamp Duty | **Lecture:** 6.3.2

**Concept:** GST is on brokerage; stamp duty is on the transfer of shares.

---

Stamp duty is a state government tax on the transfer of ownership of shares. It is levied on the purchase of shares. The rate varies from state to state but is typically a very small percentage (e.g., 0.015% of the turnover). It is collected by the broker and remitted to the state government. Like other costs, it is automatically deducted from your account and is a deductible expense. It is a relatively small but unavoidable cost.
