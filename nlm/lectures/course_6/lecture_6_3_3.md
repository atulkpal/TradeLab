# Lecture 6.3.3: Total Transaction Cost

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.3: GST & Stamp Duty | **Lecture:** 6.3.3

**Concept:** GST is on brokerage; stamp duty is on the transfer of shares.

---

For a retail trader, the total transaction cost is the sum of STT, brokerage, GST, stamp duty, and exchange charges. On a typical delivery trade, STT is the biggest component. On an options trade, the brokerage and exchange charges can be significant. It is important to calculate the total cost before entering a trade. For example, a 0.1% move in the stock price might be completely eaten up by these costs. This is why a favorable RRR is crucial in India.
