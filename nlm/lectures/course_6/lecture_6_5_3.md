# Lecture 6.5.3: Choosing Your Tax Status

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.5: Intraday vs Delivery Taxation | **Lecture:** 6.5.3

**Concept:** Intraday is speculative business; delivery is business or capital gains.

---

There is no clear rule for when you are a 'trader' vs. 'investor'. It is a question of fact. The Income Tax Department will look at the volume of trades, the holding period, and your intention. If you are making hundreds of trades a day, you are a trader. If you buy and hold 10 stocks a year, you are an investor. If you are in between, it's advisable to consult a Chartered Accountant (CA) to determine the best strategy for you.
