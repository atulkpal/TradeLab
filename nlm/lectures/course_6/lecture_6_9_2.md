# Lecture 6.9.2: Treatment of F&O Losses

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.9: Options & Futures Tax Treatment | **Lecture:** 6.9.2

**Concept:** F&O trading is generally treated as business income.

---

Losses from F&O trading are generally treated as business losses (non-speculative). This is because the objective of such trading is hedging or speculation, and the contracts are standardized. Unlike intraday equity trading, F&O trading is not considered 'speculative'. This means the losses can be set off against any other income, including salary or rental income, making it more tax-friendly. This is tax information, not professional advice.
