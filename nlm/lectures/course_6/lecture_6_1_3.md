# Lecture 6.1.3: What Determines Your Status

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.1: Trading vs Investing - Different Tax Treatments | **Lecture:** 6.1.3

**Concept:** Trading profits are business income; investing profits are capital gains.

---

There is no single threshold. The Income Tax Department looks at factors like the volume of trades, the holding period of your investments, and your intention. An investor who holds 5 stocks for 3 years is clearly an investor. A trader who makes 50 intraday trades a day is clearly a trader. In between, the determination is based on the facts of the case. Many Indian traders choose to treat their income as business income to claim expenses. It's advisable to consult a Chartered Accountant for clarity.
