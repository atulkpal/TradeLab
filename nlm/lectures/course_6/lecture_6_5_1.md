# Lecture 6.5.1: Intraday Trading as Speculative Business

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.5: Intraday vs Delivery Taxation | **Lecture:** 6.5.1

**Concept:** Intraday is speculative business; delivery is business or capital gains.

---

In the eyes of the Income Tax Department, intraday trading is considered a 'speculative business'. Even if you treat trading as a business, intraday losses are classified as 'speculative business loss'. This loss can only be set off against speculative gains. It cannot be set off against other business income. This is a crucial and often misunderstood rule. This makes intraday trading riskier from a tax perspective. This is tax information, not professional advice.
