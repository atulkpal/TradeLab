# Lecture 6.3.1: GST on Trading

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.3: GST & Stamp Duty | **Lecture:** 6.3.1

**Concept:** GST is on brokerage; stamp duty is on the transfer of shares.

---

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive indirect tax. In the context of trading, GST is levied on the brokerage and other services provided by the broker. The rate is 18% of the brokerage amount. This cost is borne by the trader. It is a smaller component compared to STT but still adds up over many trades. This is why choosing a broker with low brokerage is beneficial. This is tax information, not professional advice.
