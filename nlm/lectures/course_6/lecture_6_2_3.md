# Lecture 6.2.3: Deductibility of These Costs

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.2: STT, Brokerage & Transaction Charges | **Lecture:** 6.2.3

**Concept:** STT is a tax on sale; brokerage is the fee to the broker.

---

All of these costs-STT, brokerage, GST, and exchange charges-are deductible from your taxable income. If you are a trader (business income), you can deduct them as business expenses. If you are an investor (capital gains), they are deducted from the sale proceeds when calculating your capital gains. This is why maintaining a proper record of all your trades is essential. Every cost reduces your tax liability. This is tax information, not professional advice.
