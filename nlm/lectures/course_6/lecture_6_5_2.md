# Lecture 6.5.2: Delivery Trading as Business or Capital Gains

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.5: Intraday vs Delivery Taxation | **Lecture:** 6.5.2

**Concept:** Intraday is speculative business; delivery is business or capital gains.

---

Delivery trading, where you buy and hold for more than a day, can be taxed in two ways. If you are a trader, the income is treated as 'business income' (non-speculative). If you are an investor, it is treated as 'capital gains'. The choice between the two depends on your overall trading pattern. Once you choose a treatment, it is advisable to be consistent. Many traders opt for business income to claim expenses. This is tax information, not professional advice.
