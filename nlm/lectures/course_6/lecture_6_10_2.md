# Lecture 6.10.2: Misreporting Turnover

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.10: Common Filing Mistakes | **Lecture:** 6.10.2

**Concept:** Avoid common errors to prevent tax notices.

---

Calculating turnover incorrectly is another major error. Many traders confuse turnover with profit. This leads to incorrect tax computations and potential penalties. For example, in F&O, the turnover is the sum of the positive and negative differences, not the total of all trades. It is vital to understand the specific rules for calculating turnover for each segment. This is tax information, not professional advice.
