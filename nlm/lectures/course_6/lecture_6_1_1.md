# Lecture 6.1.1: The Distinction in Income Tax

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.1: Trading vs Investing - Different Tax Treatments | **Lecture:** 6.1.1

**Concept:** Trading profits are business income; investing profits are capital gains.

---

The Income Tax Act of India treats trading and investing differently. If your primary intent is to make a profit from short-term price movements, your income is considered 'Business Income'. If you hold stocks as a long-term investment, the profit is 'Capital Gains'. This distinction is crucial as it affects how you file your returns and what deductions you can claim. Frequency of trades, the holding period, and your overall portfolio activity determine your status. This is a fundamental concept for every Indian trader and investor.
