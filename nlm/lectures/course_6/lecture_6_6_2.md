# Lecture 6.6.2: The Importance of Bookkeeping

**Course:** 6 | **Chapter:** Chapter 6.6: Turnover & Bookkeeping for Traders | **Lecture:** 6.6.2

**Concept:** Turnover is not profit; proper bookkeeping is mandatory.

---

Maintaining proper books of accounts is mandatory if you are a trader. This means you need to record every single transaction, along with the associated costs. A CA will require this data to prepare your income tax return. You can use a simple spreadsheet or dedicated accounting software. The books must show your purchase, sale, profit/loss, and expenses. Without proper records, you will face difficulties during an assessment.
