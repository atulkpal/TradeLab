# Lecture 1.10.1: Brokerage and STT

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.10: Costs of Trading | **Lecture:** 1.10.1

**Concept:** Trading costs include brokerage, STT, GST, and stamp duty.

---

Every trade incurs costs that reduce net profit. The first major cost is brokerage - a fee charged by your broker for executing the trade. It can be a flat fee per order or a percentage of the trade value. The second is the Securities Transaction Tax (STT), levied by the government on every sale of securities. STT is a significant cost, especially for traders. It's automatically deducted from your account at the time of the transaction. Understanding these costs is essential for calculating your true net profit or loss.
