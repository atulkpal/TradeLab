# Lecture 1.8.3: Timeframes and Candlesticks

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.8: Candlestick Anatomy | **Lecture:** 1.8.3

**Concept:** Candlesticks show price action with body and wicks.

---

Candlesticks can represent various timeframes. A daily candlestick represents one full trading day. An hourly candlestick shows one hour of action. A 15-minute candlestick shows 15 minutes of action. In India, traders select the timeframe that suits their trading style. Long-term investors focus on daily or weekly candlesticks to gauge the macro trend, while intraday traders use 5-minute or 15-minute candles for quick decisions. The same pattern on a smaller timeframe is generally more volatile.
