# Lecture 1.3.3: Sectoral and Broad Market Indices

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.3: Stock Exchanges & Indices | **Lecture:** 1.3.3

**Concept:** Exchanges are marketplaces; indices track market performance.

---

Beyond headline indices, there are many others for specific themes. The Bank Nifty tracks the banking sector, a critical part of the Indian economy. Sectoral indices like Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma, and Nifty Auto allow investors to gauge the health of specific industries. There are also broader market indices like the Nifty 500, which covers a wider universe of companies. These help investors understand if a market rally is being driven by a few large companies or if it's a broad-based recovery across all sectors.
