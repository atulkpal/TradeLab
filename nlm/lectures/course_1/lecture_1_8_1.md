# Lecture 1.8.1: The Structure of a Candlestick

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.8: Candlestick Anatomy | **Lecture:** 1.8.1

**Concept:** Candlesticks show price action with body and wicks.

---

A candlestick is a visual representation of price movement within a specific timeframe. It consists of a 'body' and 'wicks' (also called shadows). The body represents the range between the opening and closing prices. If the close is higher than the open, the body is typically green (or hollow), indicating a bullish move. If the close is lower than the open, the body is red (or filled), indicating a bearish move. The thin lines extending above and below the body are the wicks, representing the session's high and low prices.
