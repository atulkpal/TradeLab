# Lecture 1.11.3: Why It Matters More Than Stock Selection

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.11: Position Sizing | **Lecture:** 1.11.3

**Concept:** Position sizing helps control risk by limiting the capital per trade.

---

Many traders focus solely on picking the 'right' stock. However, a disciplined trader knows that position sizing is equally, if not more, important. Even with a 70% win rate, a few large losses can wipe out profits if position sizes are too large. By keeping each position small, you ensure no single trade can significantly damage your account. This allows you to survive drawdowns and stay in the game long enough to benefit from winning trades.
