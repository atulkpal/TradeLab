# Lecture 1.4.1: The Role of a Broker in India

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.4: Brokers, Demat & Trading Accounts | **Lecture:** 1.4.1

**Concept:** A broker is your intermediary; accounts are for holding and trading.

---

You cannot buy or sell shares directly on the stock exchange. You need an intermediary called a stockbroker. The broker acts as a licensed agent who places your buy and sell orders on the exchange. In India, there are traditional full-service brokers like Motilal Oswal and ICICI Direct offering research and advisory, and discount brokers like Zerodha and Groww who charge lower fees. The broker earns through brokerage fees (a commission on your trades). Choosing the right broker is a crucial first step. SEBI regulates all brokers to protect investor interests.
