# Lecture 1.12.1: The Concept of Diversification

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.12: Diversification Across Sectors | **Lecture:** 1.0.1

**Concept:** Diversification reduces risk by spreading investments across sectors.

---

Diversification is a risk management strategy that involves spreading investments across various sectors and asset classes. The idea is simple: 'Don't put all your eggs in one basket.' If your entire portfolio is invested in IT stocks and the sector faces a downturn, your whole investment suffers. However, if you also hold healthcare, finance, and FMCG stocks, a downturn in IT might be offset by gains in other sectors. Diversification helps smooth out portfolio volatility.
