# Lecture 1.2.3: Why Some Companies Stay Private

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.2: Why Companies Go Public & IPOs | **Lecture:** 1.2.3

**Concept:** An IPO is the first sale of stock to the public to raise capital.

---

Going public isn't for everyone. Many successful Indian companies remain private to avoid heavy regulatory compliance and public scrutiny. Family-owned businesses like the Ambanis, Tatas, and Birlas often prefer private structures to maintain control. Going public means sharing decision-making with thousands of unknown shareholders and meeting quarterly earning expectations. Companies may also choose private equity funding or bank loans instead of an IPO. The decision to go public is a major strategic choice that depends on the company's long-term vision.
