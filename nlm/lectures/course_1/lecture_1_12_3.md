# Lecture 1.12.3: Mutual Funds as a Diversification Tool

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.12: Diversification Across Sectors | **Lecture:** 1.12.3

**Concept:** Diversification reduces risk by spreading investments across sectors.

---

For many beginners, achieving diversification can be difficult due to limited capital. Mutual funds, particularly equity diversified mutual funds, are an excellent solution. They pool money from many investors to buy a large basket of stocks across different sectors, providing instant diversification. An index fund like one tracking the Nifty 50 gives you exposure to 50 companies in a single investment. This is a cost-effective and practical way to build a diversified foundation for your portfolio.
