# Lecture 1.7.3: Market Capitalization

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.7: Reading a Stock Quote | **Lecture:** 1.7.3

**Concept:** The stock quote shows OHLC, volume, and market cap.

---

Market capitalization (market cap) represents the total value of a company's outstanding shares. It's calculated by multiplying the current stock price by the total number of shares outstanding. In India, companies are classified into large-cap (Nifty 50 stocks), mid-cap, and small-cap. Large-cap stocks like Reliance and TCS are more stable and established, while small-caps offer higher growth potential but with higher risk. Understanding market cap helps you assess a company's size and influence within the market.
