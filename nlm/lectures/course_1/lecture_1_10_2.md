# Lecture 1.10.2: GST, Stamp Duty, and SEBI Charges

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.10: Costs of Trading | **Lecture:** 1.10.2

**Concept:** Trading costs include brokerage, STT, GST, and stamp duty.

---

Beyond brokerage and STT, there are other charges. Goods and Services Tax (GST) is applicable on the brokerage charges. Stamp duty is a state government tax on the transfer of shares, applied at a very small percentage. There are also SEBI charges (a regulatory fee) and transaction charges levied by the exchanges themselves (NSE/BSE). These 'hidden' costs, while small individually, add up over hundreds of trades. It's crucial to consider the total 'cost of trading' before deciding to enter a trade, as they eat into potential profits.
