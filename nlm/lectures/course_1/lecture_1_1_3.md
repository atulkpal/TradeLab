# Lecture 1.1.3: Shareholder Rights in India

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.1: What is a Stock? | **Lecture:** 1.1.3

**Concept:** A share represents fractional ownership in a company.

---

Being a shareholder comes with important rights. The most significant is voting rights - you can vote on company matters like electing board members or approving mergers. In India, voting is increasingly done electronically through the National Securities Depository Limited (NSDL) or Central Depository Services Limited (CDSL) platforms. You also have the right to receive annual reports and attend shareholder meetings. While 100 shares might seem small, collective shareholder power influences company governance and strategic decisions.
