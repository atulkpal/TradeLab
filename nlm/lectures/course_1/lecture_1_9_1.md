# Lecture 1.9.1: The Core Trading Hours

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.9: Market Hours & Trading Sessions | **Lecture:** 1.9.1

**Concept:** Trading is from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST.

---

The Indian stock market operates with a fixed schedule. The core trading session is from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday. The day begins with a pre-open session from 9:00 AM to 9:15 AM. This session allows for order entry and price discovery based on the volume-weighted average price (VWAP). The market then opens for continuous trading at 9:15 AM. The last half-hour, from 3:00 PM to 3:30 PM, often sees increased volatility as institutions square off their positions before the market closes.
