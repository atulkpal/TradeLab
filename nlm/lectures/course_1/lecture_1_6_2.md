# Lecture 1.6.2: Intraday vs. Delivery Trades

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.6: Settlement Cycles | **Lecture:** 1.6.2

**Concept:** T+1 means shares and cash are exchanged one day after the trade.

---

Understanding the difference between intraday and delivery trades is crucial. A delivery trade means you buy shares intending to hold them for more than one day. Shares are delivered to your Demat account via the T+1 cycle. An intraday trade involves buying and selling the same stock on the same day. Positions are automatically squared off before the market closes (3:30 PM), and the net profit or loss is settled in your cash account. There is no transfer of shares to Demat for intraday trades.
