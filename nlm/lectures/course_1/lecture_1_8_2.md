# Lecture 1.8.2: Interpreting Body and Wicks

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.8: Candlestick Anatomy | **Lecture:** 1.8.2

**Concept:** Candlesticks show price action with body and wicks.

---

The length of the body and wicks tells a story about market sentiment. A long green body indicates strong buying pressure - buyers were in control from open to close. A long red body shows strong selling pressure. Long upper wicks suggest buyers tried to push the price higher but were rejected by sellers, which can be a sign of a reversal. Conversely, long lower wicks indicate sellers tried to push the price down but were rejected by buyers. A 'doji' is a special candlestick with a very small body, suggesting indecision in the market.
