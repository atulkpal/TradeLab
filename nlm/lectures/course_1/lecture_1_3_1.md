# Lecture 1.3.1: NSE & BSE - India's Twin Pillars

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.3: Stock Exchanges & Indices | **Lecture:** 1.3.1

**Concept:** Exchanges are marketplaces; indices track market performance.

---

Stock exchanges are where buyers and sellers come together to trade securities. In India, the two primary exchanges are the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE). BSE, Asia's oldest stock exchange, was established in 1875. NSE, founded in 1992, revolutionized Indian trading with electronic systems. While both exchanges host thousands of companies, NSE generally has higher trading volumes, especially in derivatives. The Nifty 50 and the Sensex are their respective flagship indices, representing the pulse of the Indian economy.
