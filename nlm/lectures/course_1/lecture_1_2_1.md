# Lecture 1.2.1: The Capital Raising Process

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.2: Why Companies Go Public & IPOs | **Lecture:** 1.2.1

**Concept:** An IPO is the first sale of stock to the public to raise capital.

---

Private companies often need large amounts of money to expand, pay debt, or invest in projects. One major way is going public through an Initial Public Offering (IPO). An IPO transforms a privately held company into a publicly traded one. In India, companies like Zomato, Paytm, and Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) have recently gone public. The company sells a portion of shares to the public for the first time, raising substantial capital. Merchant bankers help set the price and ensure regulatory compliance with SEBI.
