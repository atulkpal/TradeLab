# Lecture 1.5.3: GTT (Good Till Triggered) Orders

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.5: Placing Orders | **Lecture:** 1.5.3

**Concept:** Different orders execute at specific price and time conditions.

---

GTT orders are a valuable innovation from Indian brokers like Zerodha. A GTT order allows you to set a price trigger condition for a limit order. The order stays valid for a year or until triggered and executed. Once triggered when the market price hits your set price, it converts into a limit order.
