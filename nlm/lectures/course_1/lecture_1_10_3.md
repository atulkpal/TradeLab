# Lecture 1.10.3: Impact on Profitability

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.10: Costs of Trading | **Lecture:** 1.10.3

**Concept:** Trading costs include brokerage, STT, GST, and stamp duty.

---

The cumulative effect of these charges can be substantial. For a short-term trader, these costs can turn a winning trade into a losing one. That's why position sizing and risk-to-reward ratio are so important. A successful trader doesn't just look at price movement; they calculate net gain after all costs. In the Indian market, STT is often the largest component. Knowing your exact costs allows you to set realistic profit targets and is a fundamental part of trading discipline and risk management.
