# Lecture 1.7.2: Volume and 52-Week Range

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.7: Reading a Stock Quote | **Lecture:** 1.7.2

**Concept:** The stock quote shows OHLC, volume, and market cap.

---

Volume is the total number of shares traded during the day. It measures market activity and interest in a stock. A stock moving up on high volume is generally stronger than a move on low volume. The '52-week range' shows the highest and lowest price the stock has traded at in the last year. This helps you understand where the current price sits historically. If a stock trades near its 52-week high, it shows bullish strength; near its 52-week low, it might be oversold or facing challenges.
