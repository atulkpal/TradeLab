# Lecture 1.12.2: Sectoral Correlations

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.12: Diversification Across Sectors | **Lecture:** 1.0.2

**Concept:** Diversification reduces risk by spreading investments across sectors.

---

When diversifying, it's not just about having many stocks - it's about having stocks from different sectors. Sectors like IT, Banking, Auto, Pharma, and FMCG often react differently to economic news. For example, high oil prices might negatively impact the auto sector but can be neutral for IT. By understanding these sectoral correlations, you can create a balanced portfolio. A well-diversified portfolio is less likely to experience a sharp decline because different sectors are rarely affected by the same event in the same way.
