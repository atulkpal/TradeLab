# Lecture 1.4.2: The Demat Account Explained

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.4: Brokers, Demat & Trading Accounts | **Lecture:** 1.4.2

**Concept:** A broker is your intermediary; accounts are for holding and trading.

---

A Demat Account (short for Dematerialized Account) is where your shares are held in electronic form. Think of it as a bank account for your stocks and mutual funds. Before dematerialization, shares were held in physical paper certificates, which were cumbersome and prone to forgery. Now, all holdings are in a secure digital format with a Depository Participant (DP). In India, CDSL and NSDL are the two main depositories. A Demat account is mandatory for trading and provides a safe way to view and track your investments.
