# Lecture 1.9.2: The Pre-Open Session

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.9: Market Hours & Trading Sessions | **Lecture:** 1.9.2

**Concept:** Trading is from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST.

---

The pre-open session is a crucial 15-minute window used to determine the opening price for the day. During this time, orders are collected but not executed until the close of the session. After 9:15 AM, the market opens based on a price that maximizes the volume of trades. This helps mitigate extreme price gaps due to overnight news. The session is split into an order entry period and a matching period. It provides a fair and transparent way to start the day's trading.
