# Lecture 1.6.3: Understanding Holdings

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.6: Settlement Cycles | **Lecture:** 1.6.3

**Concept:** T+1 means shares and cash are exchanged one day after the trade.

---

Your 'holdings' represent the stocks you own in your Demat account, typically long-term investments. The settlement cycle ensures these are safely added or removed from your portfolio. Once a stock is in your holdings, you have the right to keep it indefinitely - equity shares don't have expiry dates. Your holdings are the cornerstone of long-term wealth. Tracking holdings and their value over time is essential to any investment strategy, allowing you to see the true performance of your portfolio.
