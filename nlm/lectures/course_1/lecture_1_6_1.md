# Lecture 1.6.1: The T+1 Settlement Cycle in India

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.6: Settlement Cycles | **Lecture:** 1.6.1

**Concept:** T+1 means shares and cash are exchanged one day after the trade.

---

Settlement is the process of transferring shares from the seller's Demat account to the buyer's Demat account and funds from the buyer's account to the seller's account. India follows a T+1 settlement cycle. 'T' is the day the trade is executed, and '+1' means settlement occurs on the next working day. If you buy shares on Tuesday, they'll be credited to your Demat account by Wednesday evening. This system, introduced by SEBI, reduces risk and ensures transactions are completed efficiently.
