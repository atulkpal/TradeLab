# Lecture 1.5.1: Market Orders

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.5: Placing Orders | **Lecture:** 1.5.1

**Concept:** Different orders execute at specific price and time conditions.

---

A market order is the simplest type of order. When you place a market order, you instruct your broker to buy or sell the stock immediately at the best available current price. The advantage is guaranteed execution. The disadvantage is that you don't control the exact price - in a fast-moving market, you might get a different price than what you saw (slippage). Market orders work best for highly liquid stocks where the bid-ask spread is narrow, such as Reliance, TCS, or HDFC Bank.
