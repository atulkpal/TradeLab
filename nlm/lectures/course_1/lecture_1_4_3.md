# Lecture 1.4.3: The Trading Account - Your Interface

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.4: Brokers, Demat & Trading Accounts | **Lecture:** 1.4.3

**Concept:** A broker is your intermediary; accounts are for holding and trading.

---

While a Demat account holds securities, a trading account allows you to execute buy and sell orders. When you place a buy order, funds are debited from your bank account via the trading account, and shares are credited to your Demat account. When you sell, shares are debited from your Demat account, and funds are credited to your bank account. This seamless interaction between your bank, trading, and Demat accounts makes modern electronic trading possible. In India, these are often provided as a single integrated service by brokers like Zerodha, Upstox, and Angel One.
