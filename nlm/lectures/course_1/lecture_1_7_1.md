# Lecture 1.7.1: Price Data (OHLC)

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.7: Reading a Stock Quote | **Lecture:** 1.7.1

**Concept:** The stock quote shows OHLC, volume, and market cap.

---

A stock quote includes the Open, High, Low, and Close (OHLC) prices. The 'Open' is the price when the market opened at 9:15 AM IST. The 'High' and 'Low' represent the highest and lowest prices touched during the trading session. The 'Close' is the price at the market's close at 3:30 PM IST. In India, this data is available in real-time on platforms like NSE and BSE websites. OHLC provides a quick snapshot of volatility and price action, forming the foundation for many technical analysis tools.
