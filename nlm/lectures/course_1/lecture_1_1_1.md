# Lecture 1.1.1: Fractional Ownership Explained

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.1: What is a Stock? | **Lecture:** 1.1.1

**Concept:** A share represents fractional ownership in a company.

---

When you buy a stock, you are buying a tiny slice of a real business. This is known as fractional ownership. If a company has 10,000 shares and you own one, you are a 1/10,000th owner. This entitles you to a proportionate share of the company's profits and assets. As the company grows, the value of your slice increases. In India, companies like Reliance Industries have millions of shares, and owning even 100 shares makes you a part-owner of India's largest conglomerate. This ownership is what makes stocks a powerful wealth-building tool.
