# Lecture 1.9.3: Trading Holidays & Calendar

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.9: Market Hours & Trading Sessions | **Lecture:** 1.9.3

**Concept:** Trading is from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST.

---

The stock exchanges are closed on national holidays and significant festivals. The NSE and BSE have a predefined holiday calendar published at the start of each year. It's important to be aware of these holidays to avoid unexpected market closures. Common holidays include Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali (Muhurat trading is a special one-hour session), and Christmas. Planning your trades around the holiday calendar is a basic but essential aspect of market discipline.
