# Lecture 1.3.2: Understanding Nifty 50 & Sensex

**Course:** 1 | **Chapter:** Chapter 1.3: Stock Exchanges & Indices | **Lecture:** 1.3.2

**Concept:** Exchanges are marketplaces; indices track market performance.

---

An index is a basket of stocks used to measure market performance. The Nifty 50 is the flagship index of the NSE, comprising the top 50 companies listed on the exchange. It represents the performance of India's largest corporations. The BSE Sensex consists of 30 well-established and financially sound companies. When the news says 'the market is up,' it's usually referring to a rise in the Nifty 50 or the Sensex. These indices act as barometers for the overall health of the Indian stock market and are tracked by investors worldwide.
