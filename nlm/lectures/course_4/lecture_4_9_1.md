# Lecture 4.9.1: The Covered Call Strategy

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.9: Option Strategies - Covered Call, Bull Call Spread | **Lecture:** 4.9.1

**Concept:** Combining options can reduce risk or cost.

---

A Covered Call strategy involves owning the underlying stock and selling (writing) a Call option against it. This is a bullish-to-neutral strategy. It generates income (the premium) and provides a small downside buffer. In India, this is a popular strategy for stock holders looking to generate extra income. However, if the stock price rises sharply, your upside is limited to the strike price. It's a way to 'rent out' your stock.
