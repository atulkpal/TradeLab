# Lecture 4.4.1: The Cost of Carry Model

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.4: Futures Pricing & Mark-to-Market | **Lecture:** 4.4.1

**Concept:** Futures price = Spot + Cost of Carry; MTM is daily settlement.

---

Futures prices are not arbitrary. They are determined by the 'Cost of Carry' model. The formula is: Futures Price = Spot Price + Cost of Carry. The cost of carry includes the interest cost (risk-free rate) and storage costs (for commodities), minus any income generated (like dividends for stocks). This model suggests that the futures price is generally higher than the spot price in a normal market, a condition known as 'contango'. In India, index futures are typically in contango.
