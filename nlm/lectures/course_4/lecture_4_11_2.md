# Lecture 4.11.2: How Auto-Liquidation Works

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.11: Risks of Leverage & Auto-Liquidation | **Lecture:** 4.11.2

**Concept:** Leverage can wipe out capital; auto-liquidation is your safety net.

---

Brokers in India use risk management systems to protect themselves and you. If your mark-to-market loss eats into your margin and reaches a threshold (usually 60-80%), the system will automatically square off your positions. This is known as auto-liquidation. It ensures you don't lose more than you have in your account. However, it can happen very quickly, often at the worst possible price. This is why you must always monitor your margin and avoid over-leveraging.
