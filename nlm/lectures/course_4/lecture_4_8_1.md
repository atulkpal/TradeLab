# Lecture 4.8.1: What are the Greeks?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.8: The Greeks - Delta (Intro) | **Lecture:** 4.8.1

**Concept:** Greeks measure risk factors; Delta measures price sensitivity.

---

The Greeks are a set of risk measures that help option traders understand how the price of an option responds to changes in various factors. The five main Greeks are Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, and Rho. They are essential for professional options trading. For a retail trader, a good understanding of Delta and Theta is a good starting point for managing risk. These metrics help you understand the complex behavior of options in different market conditions.
