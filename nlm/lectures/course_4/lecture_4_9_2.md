# Lecture 4.9.2: The Bull Call Spread

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.9: Option Strategies - Covered Call, Bull Call Spread | **Lecture:** 4.9.2

**Concept:** Combining options can reduce risk or cost.

---

A Bull Call Spread is a bullish strategy that involves buying a Call option at a lower strike price and selling another Call option at a higher strike price. This limits both your potential profit and your maximum loss. It is a less expensive alternative to buying a plain Call option. It is a great strategy for a moderately bullish view.
