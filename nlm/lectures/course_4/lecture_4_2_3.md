# Lecture 4.2.3: Futures vs. Spot Market

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.2: Futures Contracts Basics | **Lecture:** 4.2.3

**Concept:** Futures are agreements to buy or sell at a future date at a fixed price.

---

The spot market is where you buy and sell assets for immediate delivery (T+1 settlement). The futures market is where you agree to a trade at a future date. The futures price is often slightly higher than the spot price due to the 'cost of carry' (interest and storage costs). Futures are a leveraged product, offering higher risk and potential return compared to the spot market. They are used for both speculation and hedging. Remember: derivatives are high-risk and can lead to losses exceeding your initial investment.
