# Lecture 4.10.3: Physical Settlement vs. Cash Settlement

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.10: Expiry, Square-off & Settlement | **Lecture:** 4.10.3

**Concept:** F&O contracts expire; positions must be squared off or settled.

---

In India, index derivatives (like Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty) are settled in cash. Stock derivatives are moving towards physical settlement. This means you might have to actually buy or sell the underlying shares if the contract is held to expiry. This is a significant change and risk. It is generally advisable for retail traders to avoid physical settlement by squaring off their positions before expiry. Remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
