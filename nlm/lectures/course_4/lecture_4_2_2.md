# Lecture 4.2.2: Long vs. Short in Futures

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.2: Futures Contracts Basics | **Lecture:** 4.2.2

**Concept:** Futures are agreements to buy or sell at a future date at a fixed price.

---

In futures trading, you can be 'long' or 'short'. Going long means you are buying a futures contract, betting that the price of the underlying asset will rise. Going short means you are selling a futures contract, betting that the price will fall. In India, you can take a short position in futures, which is not possible in the spot market (where you need to own the shares to sell). This allows traders to profit from falling markets. For example, if you believe the Nifty 50 will fall, you can sell a Nifty Futures contract.
