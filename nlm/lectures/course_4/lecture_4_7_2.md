# Lecture 4.7.2: Calculating the Break-even Point

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.7: Option Payoffs & Break-even | **Lecture:** 4.7.2

**Concept:** Payoff profiles show potential profit/loss; break-even is the zero-profit point.

---

The break-even point is the underlying price at which the option buyer makes neither a profit nor a loss. For a Call option, the break-even is the Strike Price + Premium Paid. For a Put option, it is the Strike Price - Premium Paid. This is the price the asset must reach for the trade to be profitable. It helps you define realistic price targets.
