# Lecture 4.9.3: The Bear Put Spread and Other Strategies

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.9: Option Strategies - Covered Call, Bull Call Spread | **Lecture:** 4.9.3

**Concept:** Combining options can reduce risk or cost.

---

A Bear Put Spread is the bearish counterpart. You buy a Put option at a higher strike price and sell a Put option at a lower strike price. It is used when you have a moderately bearish view. There are also more complex strategies like Iron Condors and Straddles for non-directional views. For a retail trader, starting with simple strategies like these is recommended before moving to more complex ones. Remember: derivatives are high-risk and can lead to losses.
