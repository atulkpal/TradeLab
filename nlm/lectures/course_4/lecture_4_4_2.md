# Lecture 4.4.2: Understanding Mark-to-Market (MTM)

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.4: Futures Pricing & Mark-to-Market | **Lecture:** 4.4.2

**Concept:** Futures price = Spot + Cost of Carry; MTM is daily settlement.

---

Mark-to-Market (MTM) is the daily settlement process in futures trading. At the end of each trading day, all futures contracts are revalued based on the day's closing price. The difference between the entry price and the closing price is settled in cash. If you are long and the price goes up, your account is credited. If it goes down, your account is debited. This prevents the accumulation of large, unrealized losses and ensures the integrity of the system. It's why monitoring your positions daily is crucial.
