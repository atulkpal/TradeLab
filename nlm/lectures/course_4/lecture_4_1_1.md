# Lecture 4.1.1: What is a Derivative?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.1: Introduction to Derivatives | **Lecture:** 4.1.1

**Concept:** Derivatives are financial contracts whose value is derived from an underlying asset.

---

A derivative is a financial contract whose value is dependent on an underlying asset, index, or benchmark. The underlying asset can be a stock, commodity, currency, or index like the Nifty 50. In India, derivatives are traded on the NSE and BSE. The price of the derivative is derived from the price of the underlying asset. Derivatives are primarily used for two purposes: hedging (reducing risk) and speculation (betting on price movements). SEBI strictly regulates derivatives trading in India to protect retail investors.
