# Lecture 4.11.3: Protecting Yourself from Leverage

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.11: Risks of Leverage & Auto-Liquidation | **Lecture:** 4.11.3

**Concept:** Leverage can wipe out capital; auto-liquidation is your safety net.

---

The best protection is to reduce your position size. Never use the maximum leverage available. Just because your broker offers 20x leverage doesn't mean you should take it. Consider the risk as a percentage of your total capital. A good rule is to ensure that a single trade cannot lose more than 2-3% of your capital. Also, always keep a buffer of free margin in your account to avoid sudden auto-liquidation. Remember: derivatives are high-risk and can lead to losses.
