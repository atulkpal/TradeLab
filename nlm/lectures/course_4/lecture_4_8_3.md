# Lecture 4.8.3: Delta and Trading Strategies

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.8: The Greeks - Delta (Intro) | **Lecture:** 4.8.3

**Concept:** Greeks measure risk factors; Delta measures price sensitivity.

---

Delta is crucial for hedging and portfolio management. A 'Delta-neutral' strategy attempts to create a portfolio that is not sensitive to small price movements in the underlying. For a directional trader, a high Delta option is more sensitive to price movement. For example, buying a deep in-the-money call option with a Delta of 0.9 acts almost like owning the stock, but with less capital. Understanding Delta helps you choose the right option for your view. Remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
