# Lecture 4.10.2: Square-off Before Expiry

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.10: Expiry, Square-off & Settlement | **Lecture:** 4.10.2

**Concept:** F&O contracts expire; positions must be squared off or settled.

---

Most traders do not hold their positions until expiry. They 'square off' their positions by taking an opposite trade. For example, if you bought a Nifty 50 Futures contract, you would sell it to close the position. This locks in your profit or loss. Squaring off before expiry is a common risk management practice. It prevents settlement and allows you to exit a trade when you are satisfied with the outcome or want to limit a loss. Always monitor your positions as expiry approaches.
