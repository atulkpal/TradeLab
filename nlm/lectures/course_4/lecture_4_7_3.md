# Lecture 4.7.3: Payoff for Option Sellers

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.7: Option Payoffs & Break-even | **Lecture:** 4.7.3

**Concept:** Payoff profiles show potential profit/loss; break-even is the zero-profit point.

---

The payoff for an option seller is the mirror image of the buyer. A Call seller has a profit limited to the premium received but a potentially unlimited loss as the price rises. A Put seller has a profit limited to the premium but a loss that is limited to the strike price (as the underlying price cannot fall below zero). This asymmetry of risk is why option selling is considered a professional, high-risk strategy. Remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
