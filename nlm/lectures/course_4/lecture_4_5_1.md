# Lecture 4.5.1: What is a Call Option?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.5: Options: Call & Put Basics | **Lecture:** 4.5.1

**Concept:** A Call option is the right to buy; a Put option is the right to sell.

---

A Call option gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy a specific quantity of an underlying asset at a specific price (the strike price) on or before a specific date. You buy a Call option when you believe the price of the underlying asset is going to go up. The profit potential is unlimited, while the maximum loss is limited to the premium paid.
