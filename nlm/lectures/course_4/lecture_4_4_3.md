# Lecture 4.4.3: Contango and Backwardation

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.4: Futures Pricing & Mark-to-Market | **Lecture:** 4.4.3

**Concept:** Futures price = Spot + Cost of Carry; MTM is daily settlement.

---

Contango is a situation where the futures price is higher than the spot price, which is the normal condition. Backwardation is when the futures price is lower than the spot price, which is rarer and can occur when the market is in a supply deficit or facing near-term uncertainty. In India, index futures are usually in contango, but specific stock futures can sometimes be in backwardation. Understanding these concepts can give you insight into market sentiment. Remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
