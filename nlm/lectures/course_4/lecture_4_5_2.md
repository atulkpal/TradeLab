# Lecture 4.5.2: What is a Put Option?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.5: Options: Call & Put Basics | **Lecture:** 4.5.2

**Concept:** A Call option is the right to buy; a Put option is the right to sell.

---

A Put option gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to sell a specific quantity of an underlying asset at the strike price on or before expiry. You buy a Put option when you believe the price of the underlying asset is going to go down. The profit potential is limited to the strike price minus the premium, while the maximum loss is the premium paid. Put options are used for bearish outlooks or as a hedge against a portfolio's downside risk. For example, if you own Reliance shares, you can buy a Put to protect against a fall.
