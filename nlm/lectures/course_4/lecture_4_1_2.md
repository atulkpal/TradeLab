# Lecture 4.1.2: Types of Derivatives

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.1: Introduction to Derivatives | **Lecture:** 4.1.2

**Concept:** Derivatives are financial contracts whose value is derived from an underlying asset.

---

The two most common types of derivatives in India are Futures and Options. A Futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell an asset at a predetermined price on a specific future date. An Options contract gives the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell the underlying asset at a specific price on or before a specific date. Futures and options are traded for indices (Nifty 50, Bank Nifty), individual stocks (Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank), and various commodities. Each has different risk and reward profiles.
