# Lecture 4.10.1: The Expiry Date

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.10: Expiry, Square-off & Settlement | **Lecture:** 4.10.1

**Concept:** F&O contracts expire; positions must be squared off or settled.

---

Every Futures and Options contract has an expiry date. On this day, the contract ceases to exist. For monthly contracts, this is the last Thursday of the month. On expiry, the contract is settled. For futures, this is a cash settlement based on the closing price of the underlying asset. For options, contracts 'in-the-money' are automatically exercised and settled in cash. It is crucial to know the expiry date, as it influences the time value and the volatility of the contract.
