# Lecture 4.6.1: The Option Premium

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.6: Option Premium, Intrinsic & Time Value | **Lecture:** 4.6.1

**Concept:** Premium = Intrinsic Value + Time Value.

---

The option premium is the price the buyer pays to the seller for the option contract. It is influenced by several factors, including the underlying price, the strike price, time to expiry, volatility, and interest rates. The premium can be broken down into two components: intrinsic value and time value. Understanding these components is key to determining whether an option is fairly priced.
