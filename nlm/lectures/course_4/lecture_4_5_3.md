# Lecture 4.5.3: The Option Buyer vs. Seller

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.5: Options: Call & Put Basics | **Lecture:** 4.5.3

**Concept:** A Call option is the right to buy; a Put option is the right to sell.

---

The buyer of an option pays a premium and has no further financial obligation. The seller (or writer) of an option receives the premium and assumes the obligation to fulfill the contract if the buyer chooses to exercise it. Option sellers have limited profit potential (the premium) but potentially unlimited risk. As a retail trader, you are most likely to be an option buyer, given the defined risk, but many also trade as sellers to generate income. Remember: derivatives are high-risk and can lead to losses exceeding your initial investment.
