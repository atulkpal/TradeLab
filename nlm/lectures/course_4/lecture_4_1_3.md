# Lecture 4.1.3: The Role of Derivatives in India

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.1: Introduction to Derivatives | **Lecture:** 4.1.3

**Concept:** Derivatives are financial contracts whose value is derived from an underlying asset.

---

The Indian derivatives market is one of the largest in the world. It provides liquidity and price discovery. It allows large institutional investors to hedge their portfolios against market downturns. However, it also attracts retail traders seeking high returns. It is crucial to understand that derivatives are complex instruments and carry significant risk. SEBI regularly updates regulations to protect investors and ensure market stability. Always remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments and can lead to losses exceeding your initial investment.
