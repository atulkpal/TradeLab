# Lecture 4.12.3: Knowing the Difference

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.12: Hedging vs Speculation in F&O | **Lecture:** 4.12.3

**Concept:** Hedging reduces risk; speculation seeks profit from risk.

---

The line between hedging and speculation can sometimes blur. If you are hedging, your goal is not to make money from the derivative; it is to protect your existing wealth. If you are speculating, you are purely betting on price direction. It is important to be honest with yourself about which one you are doing. This distinction is the foundation of your trading plan and risk strategy. Both are valid, but they require different mindsets. Remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
