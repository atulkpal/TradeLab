# Lecture 4.3.3: Auto-Liquidation & Margin Call

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.3: Margins & Leverage | **Lecture:** 4.3.3

**Concept:** Margins are collateral; leverage multiplies both profit and loss.

---

If the market moves against your position and your margin falls below the maintenance margin level, you will receive a 'margin call' to deposit more funds. If you fail to do so, the broker will 'auto-liquidate' your position to recover the losses. This means your trade will be closed at a loss. This protects the broker and the exchange from loss but can be a painful experience for the trader. Understanding this is essential before trading in futures. Always remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
