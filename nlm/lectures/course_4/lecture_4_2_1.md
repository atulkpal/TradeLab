# Lecture 4.2.1: Mechanics of a Futures Contract

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.2: Futures Contracts Basics | **Lecture:** 4.2.1

**Concept:** Futures are agreements to buy or sell at a future date at a fixed price.

---

A futures contract is a standardized agreement to buy or sell a specific quantity of an underlying asset at a predetermined price on a set date in the future. The key elements are the underlying asset, the expiry date, and the contract size. In India, the expiry date is typically the last Thursday of the month. The contract size for an index like the Nifty 50 is fixed by the exchange (currently 50 units). Futures are marked-to-market daily, which means profits and losses are settled each day.
