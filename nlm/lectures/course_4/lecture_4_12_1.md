# Lecture 4.12.1: What is Hedging?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.12: Hedging vs Speculation in F&O | **Lecture:** 4.12.1

**Concept:** Hedging reduces risk; speculation seeks profit from risk.

---

Hedging is a risk management strategy used to offset potential losses in an investment. In the stock market, you can hedge your portfolio by buying Put options. If your portfolio falls, the gains in your put option will offset your losses. This is like buying insurance. Hedging is a prudent practice for long-term investors.
