# Lecture 4.12.2: What is Speculation?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.12: Hedging vs Speculation in F&O | **Lecture:** 4.12.2

**Concept:** Hedging reduces risk; speculation seeks profit from risk.

---

Speculation involves taking on risk with the primary objective of making a profit. This is what most retail traders do in the F&O market. They take a directional bet on the market without having an underlying holding. Speculating is essentially gambling with better odds. While it can be profitable, it is also the source of most losses. A disciplined trader speculates with a clearly defined risk and a structured plan. Always use stop-losses and position sizing when speculating.
