# Lecture 4.3.1: What is Margin?

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.3: Margins & Leverage | **Lecture:** 4.3.1

**Concept:** Margins are collateral; leverage multiplies both profit and loss.

---

Margin is the collateral required by the exchange to cover potential credit risk. You don't need to pay the full contract value to enter a futures trade. You only need to deposit a percentage of the total value, known as the 'margin'. In India, margins are set by the exchange (NSE/BSE). This is what gives futures their leverage. There are different types of margins: Initial Margin (the minimum to open a trade) and Maintenance Margin (the minimum to keep it open).
