# Lecture 4.11.1: The Double-Edged Sword

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.11: Risks of Leverage & Auto-Liquidation | **Lecture:** 4.11.1

**Concept:** Leverage can wipe out capital; auto-liquidation is your safety net.

---

Leverage is the biggest draw and the biggest risk in F&O trading. While it can magnify small profits into huge gains, it can also turn small losses into catastrophic ones. A 10x leverage means that a 10% move in the wrong direction can wipe out 100% of your capital. Many new traders fail because they underestimate the impact of leverage. It is a high-risk game that requires high skill and discipline. Always remember: derivatives are high-risk instruments.
