# Lecture 4.7.1: Understanding Option Payoffs

**Course:** 4 | **Chapter:** Chapter 4.7: Option Payoffs & Break-even | **Lecture:** 4.7.1

**Concept:** Payoff profiles show potential profit/loss; break-even is the zero-profit point.

---

An option payoff diagram is a chart showing the potential profit or loss of an option position at expiration, based on the underlying price. For a Call buyer, the payoff is zero below the strike price (limited loss), and increases linearly above the strike price (unlimited profit). For a Put buyer, the payoff increases as the price falls below the strike price, and is zero above it. Visualizing these payoffs is essential to understanding risk and reward. Always consider the risk of derivatives trading.
