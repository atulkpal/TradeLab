# Lecture 3.4.1: Revenue to Net Profit

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.4: The Income Statement (P&L) | **Lecture:** 3.4.1

**Concept:** The P&L shows the company's revenues, costs, and profits.

---

The Income Statement, or Profit and Loss statement, shows the journey from revenue to net profit. It starts with 'Revenue from Operations' (the money from its main business). From here, we deduct the 'Cost of Goods Sold' to get the 'Gross Profit'. Then, we deduct operating expenses (like employee salaries) to get 'EBITDA' (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization).
