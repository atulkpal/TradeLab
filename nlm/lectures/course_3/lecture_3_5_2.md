# Lecture 3.5.2: Free Cash Flow (FCF)

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.5: The Cash Flow Statement | **Lecture:** 3.5.2

**Concept:** The cash flow statement shows the actual cash generated and used.

---

Free Cash Flow (FCF) is one of the most important metrics for an investor. It is calculated as Operating Cash Flow minus Capital Expenditures (CAPEX). FCF is the cash a company is able to generate after spending the money required to maintain or expand its asset base. It is the cash that can be used for dividends, debt repayment, or buybacks. A company with consistent and growing FCF is often a valuable long-term investment. For example, TCS consistently generates high FCF due to its low capex requirements.
