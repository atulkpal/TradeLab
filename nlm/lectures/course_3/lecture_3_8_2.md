# Lecture 3.8.2: Return on Equity (ROE)

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.8: P/B, ROE & Return Ratios | **Lecture:** 3.8.2

**Concept:** P/B, ROE, and ROCE measure management efficiency.

---

Return on Equity (ROE) is a measure of how efficiently a company is using its shareholders' equity to generate profit. It is calculated as Net Profit / Shareholders' Equity. A high and consistent ROE is a sign of a strong, competitive business. It shows the company's ability to generate returns on the money invested by shareholders. A company like Asian Paints has consistently high ROE (around 20-25%), reflecting its strong brand and pricing power. In India, investors often look for companies with ROE above 15%.
