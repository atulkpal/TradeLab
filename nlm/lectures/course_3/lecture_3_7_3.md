# Lecture 3.7.3: The Limitations of P/E

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.7: The P/E Ratio | **Lecture:** 3.7.3

**Concept:** The P/E ratio compares the stock price to its earnings.

---

The P/E ratio has limitations. It is meaningless for companies that are losing money (negative EPS). It doesn't account for a company's debt level. A company with high debt might have a low P/E but be a risky investment. It is also not useful for comparing companies in different sectors. For example, an IT company will typically have a higher P/E than a metal company due to different growth expectations. In India, IT stocks like Infosys trade at higher P/E multiples than cyclical sectors like metals.
