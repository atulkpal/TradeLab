# Lecture 3.8.1: Price-to-Book Value (P/B)

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.8: P/B, ROE & Return Ratios | **Lecture:** 3.8.1

**Concept:** P/B, ROE, and ROCE measure management efficiency.

---

The Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio compares the market price of a company's shares to its book value (the net value of its assets minus liabilities). It is a measure of what the market values the company's net assets. A P/B below 1 can suggest the stock is undervalued. It is particularly useful for asset-heavy industries like banking and manufacturing. In India, banks like SBI or HDFC Bank are often evaluated using P/B.
