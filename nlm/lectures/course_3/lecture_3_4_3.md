# Lecture 3.4.3: Exceptional Items and One-Time Gains

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.4: The Income Statement (P&L) | **Lecture:** 3.4.3

**Concept:** The P&L shows the company's revenues, costs, and profits.

---

It is important to identify 'exceptional items' or 'one-time gains' on the P&L. These are items that are not part of the core business operations, such as a one-time profit from selling a division. A company might use these to mask a poor performance. When analyzing a business, focus on the core, recurring profits. Look at the 'Profit Before Exceptional Items' for a clearer picture of the underlying business health. For example, if a company shows a 50% profit increase but it's due to selling a property, ignore this for core business analysis.
