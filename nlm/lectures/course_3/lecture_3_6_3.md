# Lecture 3.6.3: Quality of Earnings

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.6: EPS & Net Profit Quality | **Lecture:** 3.6.3

**Concept:** EPS is profit per share; not all earnings are equal.

---

Not all earnings are equal. 'Quality of earnings' refers to how sustainable, repeatable, and cash-backed the earnings are. Earnings are considered 'low quality' if they are driven by one-time gains, aggressive accounting practices, or a decline in cash flow. High-quality earnings are generated from the core business and backed by strong cash flow. This is often measured by the FCF / Net Profit ratio. For example, a company with FCF/Net Profit consistently above 0.8 is considered to have high-quality earnings.
