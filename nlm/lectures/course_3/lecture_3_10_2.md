# Lecture 3.10.2: Types of Moats

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.10: Qualitative Factors & Economic Moats | **Lecture:** 3.10.2

**Concept:** Brand, management, and moats are key qualitative factors.

---

There are several types of moats. Intangible assets include strong brands and patents. A company like Hindustan Unilever has a strong brand moat in FMCG. Switching costs occur when it's expensive or difficult for a customer to switch to a competitor's product. For example, enterprise software companies have high switching costs. Network effects, like those of a platform like Zomato or Swiggy, are another type. The wider the moat, the more defensible the business. In India, companies with strong moats often command premium valuations.
