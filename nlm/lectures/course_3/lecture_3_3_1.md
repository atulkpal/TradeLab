# Lecture 3.3.1: The Fundamental Equation

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.3: The Balance Sheet | **Lecture:** 3.3.1

**Concept:** The balance sheet shows assets, liabilities, and shareholder equity.

---

The balance sheet is based on a simple equation: Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity. It provides a snapshot of the company's financial position at a specific point in time. Assets are what the company owns (cash, inventory, property). Liabilities are what it owes (loans, payables). Shareholders' equity is the net worth of the company, which is the remaining value for shareholders after liabilities are paid off.
