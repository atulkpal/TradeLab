# Lecture 3.2.3: Where to Find This Data

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.2: The Three Financial Statements | **Lecture:** 3.2.3

**Concept:** The balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement.

---

For Indian companies, financial statements are filed with the BSE and NSE under the 'Corporate Announcements' section. You can also find them on the company's official website under 'Investor Relations'. Additionally, many online platforms like Moneycontrol, Screener.in, and Trendlyne present this data in a user-friendly format. Access to this data is crucial for any fundamental analyst. For example, HDFC Bank's annual report is available on its website with detailed financial breakdowns.
