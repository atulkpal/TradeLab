# Lecture 3.4.2: Gross Margin and Operating Margin

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.4: The Income Statement (P&L) | **Lecture:** 3.4.2

**Concept:** The P&L shows the company's revenues, costs, and profits.

---

Two key profitability metrics from the P&L are the Gross Margin and the Operating Margin. Gross Profit Margin is (Gross Profit / Revenue) * 100. It shows how efficiently a company manages its production costs. Operating Margin is (EBITDA / Revenue) * 100. It reflects the company's operational efficiency. Consistent or expanding margins are a sign of a healthy business. In India, a company like ITC has high margins (around 40-50%), while a metals company might have lower, cyclical margins (10-15%).
