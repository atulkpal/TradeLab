# Lecture 3.2.1: The Interconnection

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.2: The Three Financial Statements | **Lecture:** 3.2.1

**Concept:** The balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement.

---

The three financial statements are the Balance Sheet, the Income Statement (P&L), and the Cash Flow Statement. They are interconnected and tell a complete story of a company's financial health. The Income Statement shows profitability, the Balance Sheet shows assets and liabilities, and the Cash Flow Statement shows the actual cash generated or used. All three together are required to make an informed investment decision. They are available on the company's website and the BSE/NSE under 'Investor Relations'.
