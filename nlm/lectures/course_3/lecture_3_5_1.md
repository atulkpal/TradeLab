# Lecture 3.5.1: The Three Categories of Cash Flow

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.5: The Cash Flow Statement | **Lecture:** 3.5.1

**Concept:** The cash flow statement shows the actual cash generated and used.

---

The Cash Flow Statement is divided into three categories: Operating, Investing, and Financing activities. Operating Cash Flow (OCF) is the cash generated from the company's core business operations. Investing Cash Flow includes cash spent on capital expenditures (buying assets) and investments. Financing Cash Flow includes cash flows from issuing debt or equity and paying dividends. A successful, healthy company usually has strong positive OCF. For example, HDFC Bank typically generates strong operating cash flow from its banking operations.
