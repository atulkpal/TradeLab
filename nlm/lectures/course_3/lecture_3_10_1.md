# Lecture 3.10.1: What is an Economic Moat?

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.10: Qualitative Factors & Economic Moats | **Lecture:** 3.10.1

**Concept:** Brand, management, and moats are key qualitative factors.

---

An economic moat is a sustainable competitive advantage that allows a company to protect its market share and profits from competitors. It is a term coined by Warren Buffett. A company with a wide moat can maintain high margins and returns on capital for a long time. This can come from strong brands (like ITC or Asian Paints), high switching costs (like software companies), or cost advantages. In India, companies like Pidilite Industries (Fevicol) have a strong moat due to their brand and distribution network.
