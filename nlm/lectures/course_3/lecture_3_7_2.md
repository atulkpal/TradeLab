# Lecture 3.7.2: Trailing vs. Forward P/E

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.7: The P/E Ratio | **Lecture:** 3.7.2

**Concept:** The P/E ratio compares the stock price to its earnings.

---

The Trailing P/E uses the EPS from the last four quarters (historical data). The Forward P/E uses the estimated EPS for the next four quarters. The Trailing P/E is factual and reliable, while the Forward P/E is predictive and subject to error. Many Indian investors look at the Trailing P/E for valuation, but they also consider the Forward P/E to gauge market expectations. If the Forward P/E is lower than the Trailing P/E, it suggests earnings are expected to grow. For example, if a stock has Trailing P/E of 30 and Forward P/E of 25, growth is expected.
