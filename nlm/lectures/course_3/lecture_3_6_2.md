# Lecture 3.6.2: Basic vs. Diluted EPS

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.6: EPS & Net Profit Quality | **Lecture:** 3.6.2

**Concept:** EPS is profit per share; not all earnings are equal.

---

There are two types of EPS: Basic and Diluted. Basic EPS uses the number of shares currently outstanding. Diluted EPS factors in all potential shares that could be created from convertible securities, options, and warrants. Diluted EPS is usually lower than Basic EPS and is considered a more conservative and realistic measure. It shows what the EPS would be if all potential shares were converted. For example, companies like Reliance often have a difference between Basic and Diluted EPS due to their employee stock option plans.
