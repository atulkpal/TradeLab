# Lecture 3.3.3: What to Look For

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.3: The Balance Sheet | **Lecture:** 3.3.3

**Concept:** The balance sheet shows assets, liabilities, and shareholder equity.

---

A key thing to look for is the debt-to-equity ratio, which shows how much debt the company is using to finance its assets. Too much debt is risky, especially in economic downturns. Another is the current ratio (current assets / current liabilities), which measures liquidity. Also, look at the quality of assets: has the company accumulated a lot of goodwill? Is inventory growing faster than sales? These can be warning signs. For example, a debt-to-equity ratio above 2 for a manufacturing company might indicate high financial risk.
