# Lecture 3.6.1: Understanding Earnings Per Share (EPS)

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.6: EPS & Net Profit Quality | **Lecture:** 3.6.1

**Concept:** EPS is profit per share; not all earnings are equal.

---

Earnings Per Share (EPS) is the portion of a company's profit allocated to each outstanding share of common stock. It is calculated as (Net Profit - Dividends on Preferred Stock) / Average Outstanding Shares. It is a key metric for valuation. A rising EPS is generally a positive sign. However, it is a simple metric and can be manipulated. An investor must look at the quality of the earnings that make up the EPS. For example, Infosys has consistently grown its EPS over the years, reflecting its strong business performance.
