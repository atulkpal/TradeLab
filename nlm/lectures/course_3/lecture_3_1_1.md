# Lecture 3.1.1: The Goal of Fundamental Analysis

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.1: What is Fundamental Analysis | **Lecture:** 3.1.1

**Concept:** Fundamental analysis assesses a company's intrinsic value.

---

Fundamental analysis is a method of evaluating a security by attempting to measure its intrinsic value. It involves examining related economic, financial, and other qualitative and quantitative factors. The goal is to find stocks that are trading at a price below their intrinsic value. This is based on the belief that over time, the market price will converge with the intrinsic value. In India, fundamental analysis is heavily used by long-term investors in companies like Reliance, TCS, and HDFC Bank.
