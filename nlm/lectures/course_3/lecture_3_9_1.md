# Lecture 3.9.1: Understanding Debt-to-Equity Ratio

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.9: Debt & Leverage | **Lecture:** 3.9.1

**Concept:** Debt-to-Equity shows leverage; high debt means higher risk.

---

The Debt-to-Equity (D/E) ratio is a key measure of financial leverage. It is calculated as Total Liabilities / Shareholders' Equity. It shows how much debt a company is using to finance its assets relative to the value of shareholders' equity. A high D/E ratio indicates that the company is heavily reliant on debt, which can amplify profits in good times but also amplify losses in bad times.
