# Lecture 3.9.2: Good Debt vs. Bad Debt

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.9: Debt & Leverage | **Lecture:** 3.9.2

**Concept:** Debt-to-Equity shows leverage; high debt means higher risk.

---

Not all debt is bad. 'Good debt' is when a company takes on debt to fund expansion or high-return projects that will generate growth. 'Bad debt' is when a company takes on debt to fund operating losses or unproductive acquisitions. A company like Reliance Industries might take on 'good debt' to expand its telecom business (Jio) or retail operations. The key is whether the return on the capital exceeds the cost of the debt. For example, if Reliance borrows at 8% and generates 15% return, it's creating value.
