# Lecture 3.8.3: Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.8: P/B, ROE & Return Ratios | **Lecture:** 3.8.3

**Concept:** P/B, ROE, and ROCE measure management efficiency.

---

Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) measures a company's profitability and efficiency in using its total capital (equity and debt). It is calculated as Earnings Before Interest and Tax (EBIT) / Capital Employed. Unlike ROE, ROCE includes debt in the calculation. It is a better indicator of the company's overall operational efficiency. A ROCE that is higher than the company's cost of capital indicates value creation. For example, a company like TCS has ROCE consistently above 40%, showing exceptional capital efficiency.
