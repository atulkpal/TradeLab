# Lecture 3.9.3: Interest Coverage Ratio

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.9: Debt & Leverage | **Lecture:** 3.9.3

**Concept:** Debt-to-Equity shows leverage; high debt means higher risk.

---

The Interest Coverage Ratio is another crucial metric for analyzing debt. It measures a company's ability to pay its interest expenses on its debt. It is calculated as Earnings Before Interest and Taxes (EBIT) / Interest Expense. A ratio above 3 is generally considered safe, while a ratio below 1.5 is a warning sign. It shows whether the company is generating enough operating profit to cover its interest payments.
