# Lecture 3.1.2: Top-Down vs. Bottom-Up Approach

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.1: What is Fundamental Analysis | **Lecture:** 3.1.2

**Concept:** Fundamental analysis assesses a company's intrinsic value.

---

There are two main approaches. The 'top-down' approach starts with the economy, then analyses the sector, and finally identifies the best companies in the sector. The 'bottom-up' approach starts with the company itself, analyzing its financials and management, irrespective of the economic backdrop. Both are valid. Many Indian investors start with a bottom-up approach, focusing on well-known companies like HDFC Bank or Reliance, before considering the broader economic context.
