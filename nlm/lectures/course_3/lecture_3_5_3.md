# Lecture 3.5.3: Cash Flow vs. Net Profit

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.5: The Cash Flow Statement | **Lecture:** 3.5.3

**Concept:** The cash flow statement shows the actual cash generated and used.

---

It is crucial to compare the cash flow from operations with the net profit. A company can show a healthy net profit on the P&L but have negative operating cash flow. This is a red flag and suggests that the profit is not 'real' cash but is tied up in receivables or inventory. A company that consistently has OCF greater than its net profit is in a very strong financial position.
