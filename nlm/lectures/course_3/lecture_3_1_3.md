# Lecture 3.1.3: Qualitative and Quantitative Factors

**Course:** 3 | **Chapter:** Chapter 3.1: What is Fundamental Analysis | **Lecture:** 3.1.3

**Concept:** Fundamental analysis assesses a company's intrinsic value.

---

Quantitative factors are the hard numbers: revenue, profit, debt, and other financial metrics. Qualitative factors are the softer aspects: the quality of the management, the brand value, the company's competitive advantage (moat), and industry trends. Both are crucial for a complete analysis. A company with strong numbers but poor management might be a bad investment, and vice versa. In India, companies like Asian Paints have strong qualitative factors through their brand moat.
