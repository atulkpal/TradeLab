# Lecture 2.8.1: Single Candlestick Patterns

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.8: Candlestick Patterns | **Lecture:** 2.8.1

**Concept:** Candlestick patterns provide clues about market sentiment.

---

Single candlestick patterns can provide strong reversal signals. The 'Hammer' is a bullish reversal pattern that forms during a downtrend. It has a small body and a long lower wick, indicating that sellers pushed the price down, but buyers stepped in to push it back up. The 'Shooting Star' is a bearish reversal pattern that forms in an uptrend. It has a small body and a long upper wick, suggesting a failed rally. The 'Doji' indicates indecision and potential reversal.
