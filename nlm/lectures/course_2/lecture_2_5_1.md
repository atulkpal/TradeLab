# Lecture 2.5.1: Understanding Moving Averages

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.5: Moving Averages - SMA & EMA | **Lecture:** 2.5.1

**Concept:** Moving averages smooth price data to identify trend direction.

---

A moving average (MA) is a widely used indicator that smooths out price data to create a single flowing line. This makes it easier to identify the direction of the trend. It helps filter out the 'noise' of short-term price fluctuations. There are two main types: the Simple Moving Average (SMA) and the Exponential Moving Average (EMA). Moving averages are often used for both trend identification and as dynamic support/resistance levels.
