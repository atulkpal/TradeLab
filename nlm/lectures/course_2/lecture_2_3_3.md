# Lecture 2.3.3: Identifying Levels

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.3: Support & Resistance | **Lecture:** 2.3.3

**Concept:** Support and resistance are price levels where reversals occur.

---

Identifying support and resistance levels is a skill developed over time. They are typically found at price points where the stock has reversed multiple times in the past. You can draw horizontal lines at these swing highs and lows. The more times a level has been tested, the stronger it becomes. In addition to horizontal lines, you can also use moving averages, trendlines, and pivot points to identify dynamic support and resistance.
