# Lecture 2.3.2: Role Reversal

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.3: Support & Resistance | **Lecture:** 2.3.2

**Concept:** Support and resistance are price levels where reversals occur.

---

One of the most powerful concepts in technical analysis is role reversal. A former resistance level, once broken, becomes a new support level. Conversely, a former support level, once broken, can become a new resistance level. This is because traders who missed the breakout may now view the old resistance as a good entry point. This phenomenon is driven by market psychology and is a key concept in identifying potential entry and exit points.
