# Lecture 2.11.3: Price Action is King

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.11: Combining Indicators | **Lecture:** 2.11.3

**Concept:** Avoid indicator soup; use a mix of different types for confirmation.

---

Remember that indicators are just tools to read price action; they are not the price action itself. The best signal is often the most simple: the price movement and volume. A strong trend with clean price action, confirmed by one or two indicators, is a much better setup than a weak trend with many contradictory signals. Focus on the chart itself and use indicators only for confirmation, not as a primary entry signal.
