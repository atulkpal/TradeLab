# Lecture 2.10.3: The 'Step Up, Step Down' Rule

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.10: Choosing the Right Timeframe | **Lecture:** 2.10.3

**Concept:** Match your timeframe to your trading style and risk tolerance.

---

A common professional approach is the 'Step Up, Step Down' rule. Step up one timeframe to determine the trend (e.g., use the weekly chart to determine the trend for your daily trades). Step down one timeframe to find your entry (e.g., use the 4-hour chart to time your entry on a daily trade). This gives you a clear macro context and a precise execution point, improving the probability of success.
