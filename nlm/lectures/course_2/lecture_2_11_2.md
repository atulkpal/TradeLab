# Lecture 2.11.2: The Three-Category Rule

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.11: Combining Indicators | **Lecture:** 2.11.2

**Concept:** Avoid indicator soup; use a mix of different types for confirmation.

---

A disciplined approach is to use a maximum of three indicators from different categories. One indicator to gauge the trend (like a moving average), one to gauge momentum (like the MACD), and one to gauge volatility or volume (like the RSI or volume). This gives you a well-rounded view. Each category provides a different piece of information, and when they align, it is a powerful signal.
