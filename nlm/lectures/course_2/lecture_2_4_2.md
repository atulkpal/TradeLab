# Lecture 2.4.2: Drawing Trendlines

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.4: Trendlines & Trend Direction | **Lecture:** 2.4.2

**Concept:** Trendlines visually identify the direction and strength of a trend.

---

A trendline is a straight line that connects two or more price points on a chart. An uptrend line is drawn by connecting the ascending swing lows (support). A downtrend line is drawn by connecting the descending swing highs (resistance). Trendlines act as dynamic support and resistance. The more times a trendline is touched without being broken, the stronger it is. When a trendline is broken, it often signals a potential trend reversal.
