# Lecture 2.1.2: Technical vs. Fundamental Analysis

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.1: What is Technical Analysis (and its limits) | **Lecture:** 2.1.2

**Concept:** Technical analysis studies past price data to forecast future moves.

---

While fundamental analysis looks at a company's financial health and intrinsic value, technical analysis is purely concerned with price action. A technical analyst doesn't care if a company's earnings are growing; they care about what the price chart says. Both approaches can be valuable. Technical analysis is often used for short-to-medium term trading, while fundamental analysis is typically used for long-term investing. Many successful traders combine both: using fundamental analysis to find good companies and technical analysis to find good entry and exit points.
