# Lecture 2.9.3: Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.9: Volume & Volume Spikes | **Lecture:** 2.9.3

**Concept:** Volume confirms price trends and signals potential breakouts.

---

VWAP is a trading benchmark that gives the average price a security has traded at throughout the day, based on both volume and price. It is calculated by taking the total value traded and dividing it by the total volume. Large institutions and traders use VWAP to measure their execution quality. If you buy below the VWAP, you did better than the average trader that day. VWAP acts as a dynamic support and resistance level for intraday traders.
