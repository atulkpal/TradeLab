# Lecture 2.8.3: Three-Candlestick Patterns

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.8: Candlestick Patterns | **Lecture:** 2.8.3

**Concept:** Candlestick patterns provide clues about market sentiment.

---

The 'Morning Star' is a bullish reversal pattern consisting of three candles: a long red candle, a small-bodied candle (doji or spinning top), and a long green candle. It indicates that selling pressure is ending. The 'Evening Star' is the bearish equivalent, starting with a long green candle, followed by a small-bodied candle, and finishing with a long red candle. These patterns are considered more reliable than single-candle patterns due to the additional confirmation.
