# Lecture 2.4.1: Identifying a Trend

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.4: Trendlines & Trend Direction | **Lecture:** 2.4.1

**Concept:** Trendlines visually identify the direction and strength of a trend.

---

A trend is the general direction in which a market is moving. There are three types: uptrend, downtrend, and sideways (or range-bound). An uptrend is characterized by a series of higher highs and higher lows. A downtrend is a series of lower highs and lower lows. A sideways trend sees the price oscillating between support and resistance levels. Recognizing the trend is the first step in trading, as it dictates the most favorable direction for your trades.
