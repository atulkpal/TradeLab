# Lecture 2.8.2: Two-Candlestick Patterns

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.8: Candlestick Patterns | **Lecture:** 2.8.2

**Concept:** Candlestick patterns provide clues about market sentiment.

---

The 'Bullish Engulfing' pattern is a strong reversal signal. It consists of a small red candle followed by a large green candle that completely engulfs the previous candle's body. It suggests a shift in control from sellers to buyers. Conversely, the 'Bearish Engulfing' pattern has a small green candle followed by a large red candle that engulfs it, signaling a shift from buyers to sellers. These patterns are highly effective when they appear at key support or resistance levels.
