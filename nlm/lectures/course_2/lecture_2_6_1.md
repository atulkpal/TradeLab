# Lecture 2.6.1: What is the RSI?

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.6: RSI - Relative Strength Index | **Lecture:** 2.6.1

**Concept:** RSI measures the magnitude of recent price changes to gauge overbought/oversold.

---

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a popular momentum oscillator that measures the speed and change of price movements. It oscillates between 0 and 100. It is used to identify overbought and oversold conditions in the market. An RSI above 70 suggests the stock is overbought, while an RSI below 30 suggests it is oversold. These levels can indicate potential trend reversals. The RSI is a leading indicator, meaning it often signals a potential change in price direction before it happens.
