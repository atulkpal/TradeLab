# Lecture 2.9.1: The Role of Volume

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.9: Volume & Volume Spikes | **Lecture:** 2.9.1

**Concept:** Volume confirms price trends and signals potential breakouts.

---

Volume is the total number of shares traded during a given period. It acts as the fuel behind a price move. A price move on low volume is considered less significant, while a move on high volume is seen as more powerful and reliable. Volume helps to confirm the strength of a trend. In an uptrend, increasing volume on up days and decreasing volume on down days is a sign of health. In a downtrend, the opposite is true.
