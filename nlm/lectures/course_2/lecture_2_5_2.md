# Lecture 2.5.2: Simple Moving Average (SMA)

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.5: Moving Averages - SMA & EMA | **Lecture:** 2.5.2

**Concept:** Moving averages smooth price data to identify trend direction.

---

The SMA is the average of the closing prices over a set number of periods. For example, a 20-day SMA is the average of the closing prices over the last 20 days. The SMA gives equal weight to each price point, which can make it somewhat slower to react to new information. It is a reliable indicator of the overall trend. The 50-day and 200-day SMAs are widely watched by Indian traders and investors for the Nifty 50.
