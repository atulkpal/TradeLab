# Lecture 2.7.3: MACD Divergence

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.7: MACD | **Lecture:** 2.7.3

**Concept:** MACD shows the relationship between two moving averages.

---

Like the RSI, the MACD can also show divergence. A bullish divergence occurs when the price makes a lower low, but the MACD makes a higher low. This is a strong signal that the bearish trend is losing momentum. A bearish divergence occurs when the price makes a higher high, but the MACD makes a lower high. This warns that the bullish trend is weakening. Divergence with the MACD is considered a very reliable signal.
