# Lecture 2.2.1: Understanding Price Action

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.2: Price Action & Timeframes | **Lecture:** 2.2.1

**Concept:** Price action is the movement of price; timeframes define the lens.

---

Price action is the movement of a security's price plotted over time. It is the raw data of the market, stripped of indicators. By observing price action, you can see the battle between buyers and sellers. A series of higher highs and higher lows indicates an uptrend, while lower highs and lower lows indicates a downtrend. Price action trading involves making decisions based on these simple observations, often using support, resistance, and trendlines. It is a foundational skill for any chartist.
