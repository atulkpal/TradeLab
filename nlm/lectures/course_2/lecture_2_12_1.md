# Lecture 2.12.1: Ignoring the Bigger Trend

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.12: Common TA Mistakes & Whipsaws | **Lecture:** 2.12.1

**Concept:** Whipsaws and false signals are the biggest challenges in TA.

---

One of the most common mistakes is ignoring the larger trend. Many traders become too focused on a smaller timeframe and take trades that go against the primary trend. This significantly reduces the probability of success. Always start your analysis by looking at the weekly or daily chart to determine the macro trend. A bullish setup on the 15-minute chart is far more powerful if the daily chart is also in an uptrend.
