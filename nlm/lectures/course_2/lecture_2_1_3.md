# Lecture 2.1.3: The Limits of Technical Analysis

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.1: What is Technical Analysis (and its limits) | **Lecture:** 2.1.3

**Concept:** Technical analysis studies past price data to forecast future moves.

---

Technical analysis is not a crystal ball. It does not guarantee profits. Markets can be irrational and unpredictable, leading to false signals. It is an art as much as a science, and two analysts can look at the same chart and draw different conclusions. It works best in liquid markets with high participation. In volatile or illiquid stocks, price patterns can be unreliable. A disciplined trader uses technical analysis as a tool for decision-making, not as a source of certainty, and always combines it with sound risk management.
