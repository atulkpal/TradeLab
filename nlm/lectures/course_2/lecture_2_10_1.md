# Lecture 2.10.1: Trading Styles and Timeframes

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.10: Choosing the Right Timeframe | **Lecture:** 2.10.1

**Concept:** Match your timeframe to your trading style and risk tolerance.

---

Your chosen timeframe should align with your trading style. Scalpers trade on 1-minute charts, holding for seconds to minutes. Day traders use 5-15 minute charts to hold positions for minutes to hours. Swing traders use 4-hour to daily charts, holding for a few days to weeks. Position traders and investors use daily and weekly charts. There is no best timeframe; only the one that best fits your personality, schedule, and risk appetite.
