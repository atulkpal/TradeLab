# Lecture 2.3.1: Defining Support and Resistance

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.3: Support & Resistance | **Lecture:** 2.3.1

**Concept:** Support and resistance are price levels where reversals occur.

---

Support is a price level where buying interest is strong enough to prevent the price from falling further. Think of it as the 'floor' for the stock. Resistance is the opposite: a price level where selling interest is strong enough to prevent the price from rising further, acting as a 'ceiling.' These levels are formed by the collective memory of market participants. When a stock hits a support level, traders remember that it bounced before and are more willing to buy.
