# Lecture 2.2.3: The Multi-Timeframe Approach

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.2: Price Action & Timeframes | **Lecture:** 2.2.3

**Concept:** Price action is the movement of price; timeframes define the lens.

---

A multi-timeframe approach involves analyzing the same stock across different chart timeframes to get a broader perspective. For example, if the daily chart shows an uptrend, you might look at the 4-hour chart to find a potential entry point during a minor pullback. This gives you confidence that your trade is aligned with the overall trend. It helps you avoid taking trades that go against the bigger picture. The Golden Rule: always trade in the direction of the larger timeframe.
