# Lecture 2.1.1: The Philosophy Behind Technical Analysis

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.1: What is Technical Analysis (and its limits) | **Lecture:** 2.1.1

**Concept:** Technical analysis studies past price data to forecast future moves.

---

Technical analysis is a method of evaluating securities by analyzing statistics generated by market activity, such as past prices and volume. It is based on three core principles: the market discounts everything (all known information is already priced in), prices move in trends, and history tends to repeat itself. Technical analysts, or chartists, believe that market psychology and human emotion are reflected in price patterns. By studying these patterns, they try to predict future price movement. It is a visual and data-driven approach to trading.
