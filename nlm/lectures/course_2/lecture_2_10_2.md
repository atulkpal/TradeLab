# Lecture 2.10.2: The Cost of Timeframe Mismatch

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.10: Choosing the Right Timeframe | **Lecture:** 2.10.2

**Concept:** Match your timeframe to your trading style and risk tolerance.

---

Mismatching your timeframe to your strategy is a common mistake. Using a 5-minute chart for a decision that should be made on a daily chart can lead to overtrading and premature exits. Conversely, using a daily chart for an intraday trade can result in taking positions that are not aligned with the short-term momentum. It leads to confusion and poor risk-reward outcomes. Stick to one primary timeframe for your analysis.
