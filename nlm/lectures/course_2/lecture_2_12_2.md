# Lecture 2.12.2: Overconfidence in False Signals (Whipsaws)

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.12: Common TA Mistakes & Whipsaws | **Lecture:** 2.12.2

**Concept:** Whipsaws and false signals are the biggest challenges in TA.

---

A 'whipsaw' is a situation where a trader enters a trade based on a signal that is quickly invalidated by a strong move in the opposite direction. This often happens due to false breakouts. For example, a stock might break above a resistance level on low volume, only to quickly fall back below it. This 'fakeout' traps traders. Using volume confirmation and waiting for a close above the resistance level can help filter out whipsaws.
