# Lecture 2.6.2: Interpreting RSI Signals

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.6: RSI - Relative Strength Index | **Lecture:** 2.6.2

**Concept:** RSI measures the magnitude of recent price changes to gauge overbought/oversold.

---

The RSI is not just about overbought and oversold. Divergence is a key signal. If the price makes a new high, but the RSI makes a lower high, it's called bearish divergence and suggests weakening momentum. Conversely, a bullish divergence occurs when the price makes a new low, but the RSI makes a higher low, indicating weakening selling pressure. A 'breakout' from an overbought or oversold condition is also significant.
