# Lecture 2.11.1: The Problem of Indicator Soup

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.11: Combining Indicators | **Lecture:** 2.11.1

**Concept:** Avoid indicator soup; use a mix of different types for confirmation.

---

Many traders fall into the trap of using too many indicators, resulting in 'indicator soup.' When you overlay multiple indicators, they often conflict and create confusion. For example, the RSI might signal overbought while the MACD is still bullish. This is because indicators are all derived from the same price data; they are not independent data sources. Over-reliance on indicators can lead to analysis paralysis.
