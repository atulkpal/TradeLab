# Lecture 2.6.3: RSI in Trend Analysis

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.6: RSI - Relative Strength Index | **Lecture:** 2.6.3

**Concept:** RSI measures the magnitude of recent price changes to gauge overbought/oversold.

---

In strong trends, the RSI can stay in overbought or oversold territory for an extended period. In a strong uptrend, the RSI often hovers around 60-80; falling below 40 might signal the trend is weakening. In a downtrend, it hovers around 20-40. Therefore, it's important to use the RSI in conjunction with trend analysis. A 'bullish divergence' in a downtrend is a stronger signal than one in an uptrend.
