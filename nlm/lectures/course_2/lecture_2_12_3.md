# Lecture 2.12.3: Failing to Use Stop-Loss

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.12: Common TA Mistakes & Whipsaws | **Lecture:** 2.12.3

**Concept:** Whipsaws and false signals are the biggest challenges in TA.

---

Technical analysis is not always correct. Patterns can fail, support can break, and indicators can give false signals. Failing to use a stop-loss is the single most expensive mistake a technical analyst can make. A stop-loss is your insurance policy. It limits your losses when the market proves you wrong. There is no shame in being wrong; there is only shame in not managing the risk when you are wrong.
