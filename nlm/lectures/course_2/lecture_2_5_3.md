# Lecture 2.5.3: Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.5: Moving Averages - SMA & EMA | **Lecture:** 2.5.3

**Concept:** Moving averages smooth price data to identify trend direction.

---

The EMA gives more weight to recent prices, making it more responsive to new information. This is why it is often preferred by short-term traders. It reacts more quickly to price changes than the SMA. For example, the 20-day EMA is more sensitive than the 20-day SMA. The 9-day and 21-day EMAs are popular among Indian day traders. The crossovers between different EMAs are also used as buy or sell signals.
