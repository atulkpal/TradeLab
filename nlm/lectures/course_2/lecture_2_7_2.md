# Lecture 2.7.2: The Histogram and Zero Line

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.7: MACD | **Lecture:** 2.7.2

**Concept:** MACD shows the relationship between two moving averages.

---

The histogram is a visual representation of the distance between the MACD line and its signal line. A positive histogram means the MACD is above the signal line (bullish), while a negative histogram means it's below (bearish). The zero line is a crucial level. When the MACD is above zero, it indicates bullish momentum; when below zero, it indicates bearish momentum. Crossovers around the zero line are also used as signals.
