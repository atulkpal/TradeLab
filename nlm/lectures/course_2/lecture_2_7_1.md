# Lecture 2.7.1: The MACD Line & Signal Line

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.7: MACD | **Lecture:** 2.7.1

**Concept:** MACD shows the relationship between two moving averages.

---

The Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) is a trend-following momentum indicator. It is calculated by subtracting the 26-period Exponential Moving Average (EMA) from the 12-period EMA. This result is the MACD line. A 9-period EMA of the MACD line is the 'signal line.' The interaction between these two lines generates trading signals. When the MACD crosses above the signal line, it's considered a bullish signal; when it crosses below, it's bearish.
