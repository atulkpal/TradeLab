# Lecture 2.2.2: Choosing a Timeframe

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.2: Price Action & Timeframes | **Lecture:** 2.2.2

**Concept:** Price action is the movement of price; timeframes define the lens.

---

Your trading timeframe is the period you focus on to make decisions. A long-term investor might use daily or weekly charts. A swing trader might use 4-hour or daily charts. An intraday trader will use 15-minute or 5-minute charts. There is no 'right' timeframe; it depends on your trading style and goals. It's important to remember that a trend on a smaller timeframe is often just a minor move within a trend on a larger timeframe. Understanding this hierarchy is key to making informed decisions.
