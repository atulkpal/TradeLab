# Lecture 2.9.2: Volume Spikes and Breakouts

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.9: Volume & Volume Spikes | **Lecture:** 2.9.2

**Concept:** Volume confirms price trends and signals potential breakouts.

---

A sudden surge in volume, or a volume spike, is a key event. It often accompanies breakouts above resistance or breakdowns below support. When a stock breaks above a key resistance level on significantly higher than average volume, it is a strong confirmation that the breakout is real and has conviction. Conversely, a breakout on low volume might be a 'false breakout' or a trap. Volume validates the price action.
