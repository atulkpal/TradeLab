# Lecture 2.4.3: Channels and the Angle of a Trend

**Course:** 2 | **Chapter:** Chapter 2.4: Trendlines & Trend Direction | **Lecture:** 2.4.3

**Concept:** Trendlines visually identify the direction and strength of a trend.

---

A channel is a chart pattern that consists of two parallel trendlines. It helps define the boundaries of a trend. The slope or angle of the trendline gives you an idea of the trend's strength. A very steep trendline indicates a strong but often unsustainable momentum. A moderate, consistent angle is generally healthier and more sustainable. As a trader, you want to trade in the direction of the trend, using the bottom of the channel as a buying opportunity in an uptrend.
