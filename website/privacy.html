Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

TradeLab ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our Android application and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • 01.

    Account Information: If you log in via Firebase (Google, Email, or Phone), we receive your basic profile info (name, email, profile picture). If you join the public leaderboard, your chosen display name and simulated trading scores are stored.

  • 02.

    Usage Data: We collect information about your trading simulations, quiz progress, and app interactions (via Firebase Analytics) to provide the "Learn-to-Earn" features and improve the app.

  • 03.

    Device Identifiers (Advertising ID): We collect and share your device's Advertising ID with our advertising partners (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange, served through Unity LevelPlay mediation) to display ads and measure ad performance. This is used for advertising and analytics purposes.

  • 04.

    Crash Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and diagnostic information to keep the app stable.

  • 05.

    Push Notification Token: Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a notification token so we can deliver service updates and market alerts.

  • 06.

    Purchase Information: If you subscribe to TradeLab Pro, Google Play Billing processes the purchase. We receive a subscription status — never your payment details.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data to maintain your simulated portfolio, track educational progress, process virtual capital rewards, keep the app stable (crash diagnostics), send service notifications, and display ads via Unity LevelPlay mediation (Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange) — which use device identifiers such as the Advertising ID for advertising and analytics. Subscriptions are managed via Google Play Billing. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates the following third-party services:

Google Firebase

Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics (crash diagnostics) & Cloud Messaging (push notifications)

Unity LevelPlay (Unity Ads & ironSource Exchange)

Rewarded & Interstitial Ads — collects and shares Advertising IDs for advertising and analytics

Google Play Billing

Premium Subscriptions

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Since TradeLab is a simulator, no real financial data (bank accounts or real stock holdings) is ever processed by our app.
