Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

TradeLab ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our Android application and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • 01.

    Account Information: If you log in via Firebase (Google, Email, or Phone), we receive your basic profile info (name, email, profile picture). Your email address is stored in a hashed (one-way, non-reversible) form before being used as an identifier.

  • 02.

    Usage Data: We collect information about your trading simulations, quiz progress, discipline score, and app interactions to provide the "Learn-to-Earn" features.

  • 03.

    Public Leaderboard Profile: Your display name and portfolio statistics are shared publicly on our global leaderboard. Your email address is never shared publicly.

  • 04.

    Device & Diagnostic Information: We collect device identifiers and app activity for advertising, analytics, and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).

  • 05.

    Notifications: If you enable push notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver alerts; we store a device token required to send them.

  • 06.

    Purchase Information: When you subscribe to TradeLab Pro, Google Play Billing shares purchase status with us so we can activate premium features. We do not store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data to maintain your simulated portfolio, track educational progress, and process virtual capital rewards. We use your leaderboard name and statistics to power the public global leaderboard, and we use device identifiers to personalize ads via Google AdMob (see Section 5) and to process subscriptions via Google Play Billing. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information We Share

We share only the data required to operate the app and its public features:

  • 01.

    Public Leaderboard: Your display name and portfolio statistics (total value, lifetime return, discipline score, and leaderboard rank) are visible to all users of the app.

  • 02.

    Service Providers: We share limited data with Google (Firebase, Crashlytics, AdMob, Play Billing, Cloud Messaging) strictly to provide authentication, analytics, ads, subscriptions, and notifications.

  • 03.

    Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Advertising & Personalization

We show rewarded video ads through Google AdMob so you can earn virtual in-app rewards. By default, Google may personalize these ads based on your interests and activity. You can opt out of ad personalization at any time from your device settings:

Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" / "Reset advertising ID", or toggle off personalized ads. You can also opt out of personalized advertising in the Google Ads Settings page.

Note: Opting out of personalization does not stop ads; it shows non-personalized ads instead.

6. Subscriptions & Billing

TradeLab Pro is offered as a monthly subscription with a 7-day free trial at launch pricing of ₹49/month (regular price ₹99/month) through September 1, 2026. Payments are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your card details. You can cancel at any time in the Play Store, and the subscription will not renew after the current period ends. Free-trial eligibility is determined by Google Play and is limited to one per user.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Since TradeLab is a simulator, no real financial data (bank accounts or real stock holdings) is ever processed by our app. Email addresses are hashed before use as public identifiers, and no payment details are stored by us.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only as long as your TradeLab account is active or as required for legitimate business or legal purposes. When you request account deletion, we permanently erase your Firebase account, leaderboard entry, and associated data.

You can request full account deletion at any time from the app (Profile → Delete Account) or via our dedicated page: Trade Lab Account Deletion.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at ashwathai.dev@gmail.com.
