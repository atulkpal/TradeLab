# TradeLab 2.0 — Future Vision Principles (Design Record)

I have updated the core product philosophy points as the **Future Vision Principles** for TradeLab 2.0, incorporating the latest design principles regarding identity, memories, and emotional investment.

These points are preserved in [docs/FUTURE_VISION_PRINCIPLES.md](file:///C:/Users/Atul/AndroidStudioProjects/TradeLab/docs/FUTURE_VISION_PRINCIPLES.md) and will serve as the guiding "Soul" for the next major revision of the product documentation.

### 📋 Principles Overview

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CORE PRINCIPLE**: TradeLab does not reward luck. It recognizes discipline, growth, and investing maturity. Every Arena feature should reinforce this philosophy.

> [!NOTE]
> **1. Investor Passport**: Behavior-driven identity (DNA, Titles, Ratings, History, Guilds, Certificates, Story).
> **2. Lifelong Journey**: A continuous path towards mastery, not a finite certification.
> **3. Seasons**: Alive with themes, achievements, and legacies.
> **4. Memories**: Focus on memorable moments (first win, comebacks, rivalries).
> **5. Guilds**: Belonging through communities (Colleges, Companies, Mentors).
> **6. Rivalries**: Relationships and stories built over repeated competition.
> **7. Storytelling**: TradeLab as the narrator of the user's growth arc.
> **8. Emotional Layer**: Emotional investment through Recognition, Belonging, and Legacy.
> **9. Future Optionality**: Designing for modular extensions (Sponsorships, Institutional Leagues).

### 🌟 Final Vision
**TradeLab is not merely a paper trading application. It is a lifelong platform for learning, validating, and celebrating investing mastery. Every architectural and product decision should reinforce this vision.**
